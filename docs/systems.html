Systems & Earlier Work

Before working on agents, I worked on industrial IoT, LLM products, image compression and biometric attack detection. These projects are listed from most recent to earliest. Data quality, cost and deployment requirements shaped the research choices throughout.

Industrial IoT & time-series foundation models

ChengShi Tech Ltd., Xi'an · Co-founder & Lead AI Scientist · 2023–2025

I co-founded the company and built its AI industrial IoT platform from the ground up, leading a team of 25, including 3 AI researchers. We delivered systems across several regions, serving 200,000+ residential users and 2,000+ enterprise clients. I also helped establish commercial partnerships and worked with the Series A investor, a publicly listed Chinese energy company, before moving into an advisory role.

  • Time-series foundation model with adaptive multi-scale decomposition
    Predicts demand from minute to monthly scales using a cross-temporal-scale architecture with Lipschitz constraints. Mixture-of-Experts components specialise by modality and regional consumption pattern, with transfer learning from open-domain time-series datasets.
  • Heterogeneous graph neural network
    Models users, districts or enterprises, and cities as a hierarchy, incorporating weather, building age, upstream industrial signals from coal and metal smelting, and pipeline usage history.
  • Gas leak early-warning system (40%+ → 17% false alarms)
    Replaced a baseline combining expert rules and statistics with deep-learning detection. The system is deployed in production, with both human and AI verification.
  • Demand forecasting under asymmetric loss
    Under China's energy quota system, buying too little costs 3–5× as much as buying too much, so the forecasting loss distinguishes the two errors. Preliminary results suggest 5–10% annual savings for regional gas companies. Production deployment is scheduled, with a 3-year validation plan.
  • Multimodal LLM-agent for daily maintenance
    Uses knowledge-graph-enhanced RAG across 11 independent local databases to analyse and visualise status and trends for 120,000 local users, and execute related operations automatically. It runs on the collaborator's platform.
  • Data infrastructure
    Handles 3 years of minute-level data from 200,000+ smart meters. Regional priors and pre-trained model fine-tuning address cold starts; versioned checkpoints support model lifecycle management.

Early LLM product ventures

Co-founder / principal engineer · part-time · 2021–2023

I worked on two ventures alongside my research. Both were among the earlier deployments of LLM systems in their fields; company names are omitted here.

  • Multi-agent e-commerce content pipeline
    Led a team of 7 to build a workflow from market-intelligence extraction to automated product listings. GPT-4 handled planning and decisions, while Qwen-LoRA provided lower-cost local generation. I also fine-tuned Stable Diffusion for tattoo and T-shirt designs and integrated Midjourney and Kling APIs for social-media video. People reviewed the output twice a day; the system ran autonomously between reviews. An established mid-sized e-commerce company later acquired the codebase and documentation.
  • AI-assisted report generation for a consulting practice
    Built an ESG report-generation tool for public-company compliance and competitive intelligence. It reduced first-draft time from 2–3 weeks for a team of 5 to 2 days, a 10× efficiency improvement. I managed the transition from fine-tuned BERT to GPT-3.5, balancing generation quality and training cost. I also built an annotation workflow from 2 years of reports, connecting ESG standards to report content and converting PDFs into structured training data. The delivered tool remains in internal use.

Learned image compression

Deep Render Ltd. & Imperial College London (Innovate UK) · Research Scientist / Research Associate · 2020–2021

Under Prof. William Knottenbelt, I developed a learned generative image-compression system that avoided JPEG-2000 and WebP patent restrictions and improved compression by 30%+. I independently developed a neural architecture search framework for efficient operators and a differentiable quantisation bottleneck, used internally in product line V0.1.3.

  • Three gaps for quantisation in learned image compression
    S. Pan, C. Finlay, C. Besenbruch, W. Knottenbelt
    IEEE/CVF Conference on Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition Workshops (CVPRW), 720–726 · 2021

Ph.D. — explainable biometric attack detection

University of Kent · Ph.D. Electrical Engineering · 2019

Under Prof. Farzin Deravi, I studied biometric presentation attack detection. The work combined deep learning and classical statistics with explainable AI modules and an evidence-preservation framework to improve the reliability of spoofing detection.

  • An Attention-Guided Framework for Explainable Biometric Presentation Attack Detection
    S. Pan, S. Hoque, F. Deravi
    Sensors 22(9), 3365 · 2022
