  • 2026-09-08Safety as Distance to a Boundary: A Control-Barrier Framework for Agent Trajectories

    One formal framework for the safety of an LLM agent's trajectory, built on control barrier functions: an abstract state of the observed trace, a scalar barrier h whose sign is safety and whose value is the margin, a one-step invariance condition that is the only source of a guarantee, min-composition with blame by argmin, a least-restrictive filter with a fallback, an explicit fidelity band for the abstraction error, and an adversary written as parameters. Three routes instantiate it: symbolic-compiled, environment-driven, semantic-learned. Every step is set beside its counterpart in autonomous driving (RSS, Safety Force Field, Hamilton-Jacobi reachability, STL).

  • 2026-09-08Can We Imitate the Steps by Which Humans Understand the World?

    A formal model for auto-research: an explicit state (working graph + joint posterior over a frozen candidate space), actions with frozen observation models chosen by expected information gain per cost, Bayes updates from verified evidence only, a separate gate for adopting a graph, an explicit branch for widening the hypothesis space, and a sealed, replayable end. Written out as three loops: reading, probing, benchmarking.

    auto-researchbayesianactive-learningformal-model
  • 2026-09-08Lifelong Learning, Formally: Objects, Boundaries, Schools

    A three-layer formalisation of lifelong learning: the mathematical objects (a hidden generator with a variation budget, regimes, an agent of bounded size, what its policy really holds, a bounded-capacity oracle, dynamic regret), three criteria that separate lifelong learning from eight neighbouring paradigms, and twelve schools of existing method sorted by the three challenges they answer. With the lineage of every piece, and a note on why survival, not regret, is the real score.
