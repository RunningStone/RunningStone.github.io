  • 2026-09-08Measuring Agent Safety with Control Barrier Functions

    A framework for measuring safety margins along an LLM agent trajectory. It defines an abstract state, a barrier function and a filter, then compares three ways to construct them: from tool schemas, from an environment model, or by learning. Each is discussed alongside related work in autonomous driving, with its assumptions and limits made explicit.

    agent-safetycontrol-barrier-functionformal-modelautonomous-driving
  • 2026-09-08Can We Imitate the Steps by Which Humans Understand the World?

    A formal model for an agent that reads papers, runs small experiments and evaluates candidates. It records current hypotheses and uncertainty, chooses actions by expected information gain per cost, and updates only from verified evidence. The model also separates belief updates, graph adoption and expansion of the hypothesis space.

    auto-researchbayesianactive-learningformal-model
  • 2026-09-08Lifelong Learning, Formally: Objects, Boundaries, Schools

    A three-layer formalisation of lifelong learning: the mathematical objects (a hidden generator with a variation budget, regimes, an agent of bounded size, what its policy really holds, a bounded-capacity oracle, dynamic regret), three criteria that separate lifelong learning from eight neighbouring paradigms, and twelve schools of existing method sorted by the three challenges they answer. With the lineage of every piece, and a note on why survival, not regret, is the real score.

    lifelong-learningformal-modelnon-stationaryregret
  • 2026-04-15A Company in a Box: physics, a server sandbox, and a two-tier agent team

    A sandbox for studying how LLM agents run a small ride-hailing business. It combines a street simulation, a working deployment pipeline, a shared operating budget and three roles with separate permissions. Includes four views of one run and observations about how the team makes decisions.
