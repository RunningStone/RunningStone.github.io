Can We Imitate the Steps by Which Humans Understand the World?我们能模仿人理解世界的步骤吗？

2026-09-08auto-researchbayesianactive-learningformal-model

A model of the research process怎样建模研究过程

I want a research agent to keep track of what it currently believes, how uncertain it is, and which evidence could change its view. Here I represent that state with a working causal graph $G_t$ and a joint posterior $p_t(w)$ over a finite candidate-world space $\Omega_s$. The first observation creates the initial graph and candidate space. The space stays fixed during belief updates and can later be expanded through a separate procedure.

我希望研究 agent 能记录自己当前采用的解释、有多大不确定性，以及哪些证据可能改变判断。这里用工作因果图 $G_t$ 和有限候选世界空间 $\Omega_s$ 上的联合后验 $p_t(w)$ 表示这一状态。第一次观测建立初始图和候选空间；更新信念时空间保持固定，之后可以通过单独的步骤扩展。

Reading a paper, running a probe and evaluating a benchmark candidate are each represented as an action $a$, with a fixed observation model $L_a(o\mid w)$ and declared cost $c(a)$. Within this model, the next action is selected by its expected information gain per unit cost about the current question $T$. The observation channel distinguishes reading from experimentation.

读一篇文献、运行一个探针、评估一个 benchmark 候选，都表示为动作 $a$，各自带有固定的观测模型 $L_a(o\mid w)$ 和声明成本 $c(a)$。在这个模型内，下一动作依据对当前问题 $T$ 的单位成本期望信息增益选择。文献阅读与实验通过不同的观测通道提供信息。

The update rules need to distinguish several operations. Verified, independent observations enter the posterior through Bayes. Adopting a new working graph requires a separate threshold. Adding a mechanism outside the current candidate space requires expanding that space, defining a common prior and archiving the old posterior. When the process stops, the final state is sealed so the record can be replayed without adding evidence. This makes it possible to inspect how the agent reached its conclusions.

更新规则需要区分几种操作。经过核验且相互独立的观测，通过 Bayes 更新后验；采用新的工作图，需要通过单独的阈值检查；引入现有候选空间之外的机制，则要扩展空间、定义共同先验并归档旧后验。停止后封存终态，重放记录时不再增加证据。这样才能追查 agent 是怎样得到结论的。

My take我的看法

I would like agent frameworks to make the choice between further exploration and execution more explicit. An agent needs a usable account of its uncertainty to decide whether to spend the next hour reading, testing a mechanism or acting on what it already knows.

我希望 agent 框架能更明确地处理继续探索与开始执行之间的选择。agent 需要对自己的不确定性有所记录，才能决定接下来一小时是读文献、检验机制，还是根据已有认识行动。

I think the main difficulty is obtaining the training data for this behaviour. Learning when to explore, commit or expand the hypothesis space requires varied environments and trajectories, and producing them is expensive. A single benchmark is unlikely to cover enough of those decisions. The model below is my attempt to specify the process that such data would need to teach.

我认为主要难点在训练数据。学习何时探索、何时采用已有判断、何时扩大假设空间，需要足够多样的环境和轨迹，而获取这些数据成本很高。单一 benchmark 很难覆盖这些选择。下面的模型是我对这一过程的初步描述，也用于明确未来数据需要教会模型什么。

0One loop, three observation channels一条循环，三种观察通道

Reading, probing and benchmarking use the same abstract loop with state $S_t=(G_t,p_t)$. Each round chooses an action, verifies its observation, updates the posterior and checks whether to adopt a new graph. It then continues, expands the candidate space, or stops and seals the result. The first action is fixed by the task; its observation creates the initial candidate space. The diagram describes this formal process.

文献阅读、探针实验和 benchmark 使用同一抽象循环，状态为 $S_t=(G_t,p_t)$。每轮选择动作、核验观测、更新后验，并检查是否采用新图，随后继续运行、扩展候选空间，或停止并封存结果。第一个动作由任务确定，它带来的观测用于建立初始候选空间。下图展示这一形式化过程。

box方块start起点state状态choose选动作act & observe执行并观测verify核验update更新adopt a graph?采用新图？working graph工作图extend the space扩展候选空间stop & seal停机封存

Within a fixed candidate space, select a reading action by expected information gain, verify the resulting observation and use its likelihood to update the joint posterior. A sourced proposal for a new mechanism follows the expansion procedure instead.

在固定候选空间内，按期望信息增益选择阅读动作，核验观测后用对应似然更新联合后验。有来源的新机制提案则进入空间扩展流程。

Import the reading loop's state, register an intervention and its observation model, and calibrate the measurement setup. Then select measurements by information gain and use their verified results to update the posterior.

导入文献阅读循环的状态，登记干预及其观测模型，再校准测量环境。随后按信息增益选择测量，用核验后的结果更新后验。

Public trials update the posterior and select a candidate. The selection is sealed before private conditions are revealed, and final scores are recomputed from raw paired data. Belief updates, performance history and final assessment use separate calculations.

公开试验用于更新后验、选择候选。选择封存后才揭示私有条件，最终分数从原始配对数据重新计算。信念更新、性能历史和最终评估分别计算。

L00

Intent and scope → a fixed first reading, before any state意图与范围 → 固定的第一次阅读，先于任何状态

\[a_0=\operatorname{Fixed}(\iota,\sigma),\qquad o_0\sim\operatorname{channel}(a_0)\]
\[(G_0,\Omega_0,p_0)=\operatorname{Build}(o_0;\mathcal H_0)\]

Before a posterior exists, information gain cannot select the first action. Initial intent $\iota$ and scope $\sigma$ therefore fix the first reading. Its verified extraction creates the working graph, candidate space and uniform prior. Later spaces extend this initial one.

尚无后验时，无法用信息增益选择动作，因此初始意图 $\iota$ 和范围 $\sigma$ 直接确定第一次阅读。核验后的提取结果建立工作图、候选空间和均匀先验，后续空间在此基础上扩展。

State change:状态变化： Creates the initial state.建立初始状态。

P01

Upstream snapshot as the first observation: scope, factor lineage, joint posterior上游快照作为第一次观测：范围、因子谱系、联合后验

\[o_0:=S^{\mathrm{up}}=(G^{\mathrm{up}},p^{\mathrm{up}}(W),\mathcal H^{\mathrm{up}},\mathcal L^{\mathrm{up}})\]
\[(G_0,p_0,\mathcal R_0)=\operatorname{Import}(o_0),\qquad p_0(W)=p^{\mathrm{up}}(W)\]

The first observation is a snapshot from the reading loop: graph, joint posterior over $W$, candidates and ledger. Import the posterior and already-used observation set $\mathcal R$ without resetting the prior. Accept only mechanisms with matching scope and semantics.

第一次观测来自文献阅读循环的快照，包含图、$W$ 上的联合后验、候选和账本。导入时保留后验与已使用观测集合 $\mathcal R$，不重置先验，只接收范围和语义匹配的机制。

State change:状态变化： Creates the first state of this loop from the upstream one; the upstream is read-only.由上游建出本循环的第一个状态；上游只读。

P03

Freeze the runtime, calibrate the instrument: a fixed observation before any chosen action冻结运行环境，校准仪器：任何选出的动作之前的一次固定观测

\[a_{\mathrm{cal}}=\operatorname{Fixed}(\tau,\sigma),\qquad m_b=\operatorname{median}_i T_{b,i},\qquad d=\max_b\frac{\lvert m_{b+1}-m_b\rvert}{m_b}\]
\[g_{\mathrm{cal}}=\mathbf 1\{C\ \land\ d\le\delta_{\mathrm{cal}}\}\]

The protocol fixes the calibration action before trials begin. Time the baseline in several blocks and require the largest relative drift between consecutive block medians to remain below a fixed tolerance, with every block correct. Failed calibration is recorded and blocks further trials; it does not update the mechanism belief.

协议预先规定校准动作。试验开始前，对基线分块计时，要求相邻块中位数的最大相对漂移低于固定容差，且每块结果均正确。校准失败需保留记录并阻止后续试验，但不据此更新对机制的判断。

State change:状态变化： Consumes the calibration slot; ready only on pass; no posterior change.消耗校准位；通过才就绪；后验不变。

B01

Fix the task; the first observation builds the design model, or an upstream one is imported确定任务；第一次观测建出设计模型，或导入上游模型

\[a_0=\operatorname{Fixed}(\tau,\sigma),\qquad (G_0,\Omega_0,p_0)=\operatorname{Build}(o_0;\mathcal H_0)\]
\[\text{or}\quad o_0:=S^{\mathrm{up}},\qquad p_0(W)=p^{\mathrm{up}}(W)\]

Task $\tau$ and scope $\sigma$ determine the first measurement. The initial set $\mathcal H_0$ records design assumptions, and the first verified observation creates a candidate space and prior. An upstream snapshot can instead serve as the first observation if scope and mechanism semantics match; matching node counts is insufficient.

任务 $\tau$ 和范围 $\sigma$ 确定第一次测量，初始集合 $\mathcal H_0$ 记录设计假设，首次核验观测建立候选空间与先验。也可以将上游快照作为第一次观测，但必须匹配范围和机制语义，仅有相同节点数不够。

State change:状态变化： Creates this loop's initial state.建立本循环的初始状态。

B02

Freeze the protocol and commit to the private condition冻结协议，承诺私有条件

\[\Theta=(\tau,z,K,F_0,\mathcal C),\qquad \mathcal M=(\mathcal S,\mathcal O,\mu,B)\]
\[\Pi=(\Theta,\mathcal M,h_v),\qquad h_v=\operatorname{Hash}(v,\mathrm{salt}),\qquad \operatorname{freeze}(\Pi)\prec\operatorname{trial}_1\]

Before trials, fix sizes, iterations, baseline $F_0$, candidates, seeds, pairing order, scoring rule $\mu$ and budget $B$. Commit private condition $v$ with a salted hash, allowing later disclosure but preventing selection after public results are seen.

试验前固定尺寸、迭代数、基线 $F_0$、候选、种子、配对顺序、评分规则 $\mu$ 和预算 $B$。用加盐哈希承诺私有条件 $v$，以便后续揭示，并防止看到公开结果后再选择。

State change:状态变化： Protocol frozen; private material isolated from the public policy.协议冻结；私有材料与公开策略隔离。

L09

Posterior updated, graph kept → next round后验已更新、图不改 → 下一轮

\[G_{t+1}=G_t,\qquad S_{t+1}=\operatorname{Snapshot}(G_{t+1},p_{t+1})\]
\[a_{t+1}^\star=\operatorname{Select}(S_{t+1})\]

The graph may stay unchanged while the posterior learns from evidence. Updated weights still affect the next information-gain calculation, which may select the same question again. Evidence count, posterior version and graph version are recorded separately.

工作图未变，也可能已经通过证据更新了后验。新权重会影响下一次信息增益计算，因而可能再次选择同一问题。证据数、后验版本和图版本分别记录。

State change:状态变化： Keeps the graph and refreshes the policy snapshot.保持工作图，刷新策略读取的快照。

L12

An empty valid result: record it and continue有效空结果：记录后继续

\[\mathcal E_t^{\mathrm{verified}}=\varnothing\ \Longrightarrow\ p_{t+1}=p_t,\quad G_{t+1}=G_t\]

A completed delivery can contain no supporting observation. An observation without established independence supplies no likelihood. Neither case counts as refutation. Record the attempt, acquired material and processing result separately from informative evidence.

完整交付可以不包含支持性观测，无法确认独立性的观测也不提供似然。两者都不等于反驳。尝试、获取的材料和处理结果仍需记录，并与有信息价值的证据分开计数。

State change:状态变化： Records costs and acquired material without adding evidence.记录成本与获取的材料，不增加证据。

P11

Optional commit receipt, next policy可选的采用回执，下一策略

\[S_{t+1}\longrightarrow a_{t+1}\longrightarrow\mathcal C_{t+1},\qquad m_G=0\Longrightarrow G_{t+1}=G_t\]

The next policy receives the updated direction, observation binding and catalogue. Flag $m_G$ controls whether this experimental campaign may modify the formal graph. When disabled, beliefs can change while the graph stays fixed. History transfers only when semantics and compatibility hashes match.

下一策略接收更新后的方向、观测绑定和目录。标志 $m_G$ 决定这一轮系列实验能否修改正式图；关闭时可以更新信念，但保持图不变。只有语义与兼容哈希一致时，历史才能跨轮沿用。

State change:状态变化： Information gain, catalogue and next size refreshed.刷新信息增益、目录与下一尺寸。

B08

Next policy: new action binding, next frozen size下一策略：新的动作绑定、下一冻结尺寸

\[(S_{t+1},\mathcal C_t,U_t)\longrightarrow(d_{t+1},\mathcal C_{t+1},U_{t+1})\]
\[\operatorname{alias}(c,\widehat c)\Longrightarrow\kappa(c)=\kappa(\widehat c)\]

Bind the next policy to its updated direction, catalogue and scheduled size. History keys $\kappa$ combine semantic identity and compatibility hashes. Candidates share history only with equal keys; ambiguous matches are rejected, and results from different sizes remain separate.

将下一策略绑定到新方向、目录和预定尺寸。历史键 $\kappa$ 由语义身份与兼容哈希组成；只有键相同的候选才能共享历史，有歧义时拒绝匹配，不同尺寸的结果分别保存。

State change:状态变化： Starts the next trial with the complete updated policy.使用完整的更新策略启动下一次试验。

L01

Current posterior → the confirming action当前后验 → 确认取证动作

\[\mathrm{EIG}_t(a)=H_t(T)-\bar H_t(a)\]
\[a_t^\star=\underset{a\in\mathcal A_{\mathrm{lit}}(M_s)}{\arg\max}\frac{\mathrm{EIG}_t(a)}{c(a)}\]
derivation推导
\[p_t(o\mid a)=\sum_{w\in\Omega_s}p_t(w)L_a(o\mid w)\]
\[\bar H_t(a)=\sum_{o\in\mathcal O_a}p_t(o\mid a)H_t^{a,o}(T)\]

Project the joint posterior onto the factor relevant to $T$. Use each action's fixed likelihood table to predict outcomes, then rank actions by expected entropy reduction per declared cost. The selected score must be positive. This is a pre-reading prediction of value.

将联合后验投影到问题 $T$ 对应的因子，用各动作的固定似然表预测结果分布，再按单位声明成本的期望熵下降排序。被选动作的分数必须为正；这一分数只是阅读前的价值预测。

State change:状态变化： Selects and binds an action; the posterior and the graph do not change.只选择并绑定动作；后验与工作图不变。

L02

Current graph and its last change → a discovery question当前工作图与最近一次改动 → 发现问题

\[\phi_t=\operatorname{Focus}(G_t,\Delta G_{\mathrm{last}})\]
\[Q_t^{\mathrm{disc}}=\operatorname{Request}(\phi_t)\]

Choose a bounded, deterministic search focus: newly added graph nodes and their downstream links first, then changed edges, then the original intent. At most one sourced local proposal may be returned. Hypotheses not yet in the space receive no information-gain score.

按确定规则选择范围有限的检索焦点：优先考虑新增节点及其下游连接，其次是变更的边，最后回到初始意图。最多返回一个有来源的局部提案，不为尚未进入空间的假设计算信息增益。

State change:状态变化： Sets this round's discovery focus; adds no mechanism.设定本轮的发现焦点；不新增机制。

P02

Register the experiment action: intervention, observation model, scope登记实验动作：干预、观察模型、范围

\[a=(\operatorname{do}(f),\,q,\,L_a,\,\sigma,\,c_a)\]
\[p_{\mathrm{registered}}(W)=p_0(W)\]

Register the intervention $\operatorname{do}(f)$, question $q$, fixed observation model $L_a$, scope $\sigma$ and declared cost. This identifies an available action without providing evidence for the mechanism.

登记干预 $\operatorname{do}(f)$、问题 $q$、固定观测模型 $L_a$、范围 $\sigma$ 和声明成本。这一步只标识可用动作，不为机制提供证据。

State change:状态变化： New action identity; posterior and old observations unchanged.新的动作身份；后验与旧观测不变。

P04

Direction: target entropy, information gain, cost方向：目标熵、信息增益、成本

\[I_t(a)=H_t-\sum_y p_t^a(y)\,H_t^a(y)\]
\[a_t\in\underset{a\in\mathcal A^{\mathrm{legal}}_t}{\arg\max}\ \frac{I_t(a)}{c_a}\]
derivation推导
\[H_t=H(q\mid D_t),\qquad H_t^a(y)=H(q\mid D_t,y,a)\]

As in reading, the modelling step computes expected entropy reduction for each legal action from the current posterior and fixed observation model. The policy uses these values with their action hashes and cannot assign its own information scores.

与文献阅读相同，建模步骤根据当前后验和固定观测模型，计算各合法动作预期降低的熵。策略使用附带动作哈希的结果，不能自行赋予信息分数。

State change:状态变化： Produces a direction; the posterior is untouched.产出方向；后验不动。

P05

Catalogue bound to the direction, frozen size schedule绑定方向的候选目录与冻结尺寸序列

\[\mathcal C_t=\operatorname{Catalog}(S_t,a_t,\mathcal M,z_t),\qquad z_t\in\mathcal Z=\{z_1,\dots,z_K\}\]

Rebuild the executable catalogue each round from the current snapshot, selected action, fixed method set $\mathcal M$ and scheduled size. Running an intervention at several sizes does not create several distinct methods.

每轮依据当前快照、所选动作、固定方法集 $\mathcal M$ 和预定尺寸，重建可执行目录。同一干预在不同尺寸运行，不算多种方法。

State change:状态变化： Binds the catalogue to the snapshot and keeps history separate by size.目录对应当前快照，各尺寸的历史分别保存。

B03

Snapshot, direction, mechanism action快照、方向、机制动作

\[d_t=f\!\left(G_t,\,p_t(W),\,\mathcal A^{\mathrm{legal}}_t\right),\qquad \mathcal C_t=\operatorname{Catalog}(d_t,\mathcal M,z_t)\]

Each round recomputes the direction and catalogue from the current graph, posterior and legal actions. Model identity is refreshed each round while semantic lineage is retained.

每轮根据当前图、后验与合法动作重新计算方向和目录，刷新模型身份，同时保留语义谱系。

State change:状态变化： Direction and candidate identities updated.方向与候选身份更新。

B04

Performance exploration: unmeasured first, then greedy on verified means性能探索：先测未测的，再按已验证均值贪心

\[\mathcal C^0_t\ne\varnothing\Longrightarrow c_t\in\mathcal C^0_t\]
\[\mathcal C^0_t=\varnothing\Longrightarrow c_t\in\underset{c\in\mathcal C_t}{\arg\max}\ \overline u_t(c)\ \ (\text{w.p. }1-\varepsilon)\]
derivation推导
\[\overline u_t(c)=\frac{1}{n_t(c)}\sum_{i=1}^{n_t(c)}u_i(c)\]

Measure each legal candidate once in a fixed order, then use epsilon-greedy selection on mean verified scores from compatible history. The performance policy uses no surrogate optimiser and creates no new candidates.

先按固定顺序测量每个合法候选，再对兼容历史中的核验分数均值使用 epsilon-greedy 选择。性能策略不使用代理优化器，也不生成新候选。

State change:状态变化： A measurement slot is reserved; a failure still counts.预约一个测量位；失败同样计数。

L03

Questions and scope → retrieval, full text frozen问题与范围 → 检索，全文冻结

\[\mathcal Q_t=\operatorname{dedup}(q_t^{\mathrm{conf}},q_t^{\mathrm{disc}}),\qquad 1\le\lvert\mathcal Q_t\rvert\le 2\]
\[F_t=\operatorname{Retrieve}(\mathcal Q_t,B_t)\]

Run the confirming query first, adding a discovery query when a focus is available. Before external calls, persist the reservation and read version. Save immutable copies of retrieved originals, full text and custody records. No belief update occurs yet.

先运行确认查询，有发现焦点时再追加发现查询。外部调用前保存预算预留记录与读取版本，取得材料后固定原件、全文和保管记录。此时尚不更新信念。

State change:状态变化： Adds retrieved material and source identities; the evidence already used is unchanged.新增检索材料与来源标识，已使用的证据保持不变。

L04

Frozen text and current graph → the reader冻结全文与当前图 → 阅读者

\[I_t=(G_t^{\mathrm{read}},\mathsf T_t,M_t,C_t,F_t)\]
\[\boldsymbol b_t=\operatorname{Hashes}(I_t)\]

The reader receives a fixed bundle of graph version, task, source manifest, custody records and text. Hashes identify the material read; they do not establish causal truth. The reader produces an extraction and report, without assigning likelihoods or confidence.

阅读者收到固定的图版本、任务、来源清单、保管记录和全文。哈希标识读过哪份材料，并不判断因果结论真假。阅读者只产出提取结果与报告，不赋予似然或可信度。

State change:状态变化： Produces extraction results without changing the posterior.产出提取结果，不修改后验。

P06

Plan, and reserve the attempt before running计划，并在运行前预约尝试

\[N_t=\lvert\mathcal A_t\rvert,\qquad \operatorname{start}_{t+1}\Longrightarrow N_t<B\]

Reserve and persist the attempt before execution. Failures use budget, while replay does not add another attempt. Execution also requires readiness and guard checks to pass.

执行前先预留预算并保存尝试记录。失败消耗预算，重放不新增尝试。执行还需通过就绪状态和其他前置检查。

State change:状态变化： Marks the reserved attempt as started and fixes the plan identity.将已预留的尝试标记为启动，并固定计划标识。

P07

Raw evidence: paired timings, allocation trace, correctness原始证据：配对计时、分配轨迹、正确性

\[m_{a,b}=\operatorname{median}_i T_{a,b,i}\]
\[x_b=\ln\!\left(\frac{m_{\mathrm{base},b}}{m_{\mathrm{probe},b}}\right)\]

Time baseline and probe in interleaved paired blocks with the same seed. Each block contributes a log ratio of medians in nats, positive when the probe is faster. Repeated timings within a block are not independent experiments.

使用同一种子，对基线和探针交错进行配对分块计时。每块统计量为中位数的对数比，单位为 nat，探针较快时为正。块内重复计时不视为独立实验。

State change:状态变化： Only raw evidence exists; execution success is not verification.只存在原始证据；执行成功不等于通过核验。

B05

Fixed plan: intervene on one coordinate固定计划：只干预一个坐标

\[\theta_c=\theta_{F_0}\ \text{except}\ \operatorname{do}(\theta_k:\ \theta\to\theta^\prime)\]
\[x_b=\ln\!\left(\frac{m_{F_0,b}}{m_{c,b}}\right)\]

Change exactly one coordinate relative to the baseline. Keep grid, steps, precision and inputs identical, checking them against a fixed reference. Use the probe loop's paired log-ratio statistic for each block.

相对基线只改变一个坐标，其余网格、步数、精度和输入保持一致，并对照固定参考检查。每块使用与探针循环相同的配对对数比统计量。

State change:状态变化： Immutable raw data, environment and plan hash; no posterior write.不可变的原始数据、环境和计划哈希；不写后验。

B10

Private confirmation私有确认

\[g_v=I_{c^{*}}\land I_{z,\mathcal S}\land C\land D\]
\[s_v=\begin{cases}\dfrac1m\sum_{b=1}^{m}x_b,&g_v=1\\[6pt]\bot,&g_v=0\end{cases}\]

A confirmation score is valid only if candidate identity, fixed private size and seeds, correctness and drift checks all pass. Otherwise return $\bot$, meaning no valid score rather than zero. The attempt still uses its budget slot.

候选身份、固定私有尺寸和种子、正确性与漂移检查全部通过时，确认分数才有效。否则返回 $\bot$，表示没有有效分数，不等于零分；本次尝试仍消耗预算。

State change:状态变化： The last slot is spent; a failure gives no trusted update.最后一个预算位被消耗；失败不给出可信更新。

L05

Extraction and proposal → verified against the originals提取与提案 → 对照原件核验

\[R_t=(\mathcal O_t,\mathcal P_t,s_t),\qquad\lvert\mathcal P_t\rvert\le 1\]
\[V_t=\operatorname{Verify}(R_t,I_t,r_t)\]

Check quoted spans and hashes against the originals, then verify scope, source identity, coverage, action and model bindings, and the invocation receipt. Route items to usable evidence, model admission, inconclusive or invalid. A completed delivery may contain no evidence or proposals; an unfinished delivery is not accepted.

先对照原件检查片段与哈希，再核验范围、来源身份、覆盖度、对应动作和模型，以及调用回执。各项分别进入可用证据、模型准入、不确定或无效类别。完成的交付可以没有证据或提案，未完成的交付则不予接收。

State change:状态变化： Classifies raw output; failed or inconclusive items produce no update.分类原始输出；失败或不确定项不触发更新。

P08

Verification receipt and verified score核验回执与已验证分数

\[s_t=\operatorname{median}_b x_b\]
\[r_t\notin\mathcal R_t:\ U_{t+1}=U_t\cup\{(r_t,s_t)\},\qquad r_t\in\mathcal R_t:\ U_{t+1}=U_t\]

The verifier recomputes the score from raw pairs and issues receipt $r_t$. Policy history $U$ accepts each receipt once and rejects conflicting records for the same identity. A score updates policy history, not the world-model posterior.

核验者从原始配对数据重算分数，给出回执 $r_t$。策略历史 $U$ 对每份回执只记录一次，拒绝同一身份的冲突记录。分数用于更新策略历史，不直接更新世界模型后验。

State change:状态变化： Policy history updated; the modelling state is not.策略历史更新；建模状态不变。

P09

Verified evidence: one discrete joint outcome and its log-likelihood已验证证据：一个离散联合结果及其对数似然

\[y=(z_p,z_s),\qquad \ell_h=\ln L_a(y\mid h)\]
derivation推导
\[\overline B_a=\operatorname{median}_b B_{a,b},\qquad r=1-\frac{\overline B_{\mathrm{probe}}}{\overline B_{\mathrm{base}}}\]
\[z_p=\mathbf 1\{r\ge\rho\},\qquad z_s=\begin{cases}+,&s_t\ge\delta_s\\0,&-\delta_s<s_t<\delta_s\\-,&s_t\le-\delta_s\end{cases}\]

Combine proxy change and speed into one observation, thresholding proxy reduction $r$ at $\rho$ and speed at $\pm\delta_s$. The fixed likelihood table is a design assumption and has not been empirically calibrated. A missing or unsupported proxy permits a score but no likelihood.

将代理量变化与速度合为一份观测：代理减少量 $r$ 以 $\rho$ 为阈值，速度以 $\pm\delta_s$ 为阈值。固定似然表属于设计假设，尚未经过经验校准。代理量缺失或超出支持范围时，可以给出分数，但不能给出似然。

State change:状态变化： After verification, records one acquisition and applies its likelihood once.核验通过后记录一次取材，并应用对应似然一次。

B06

Verification receipt and per-round median score核验回执与每轮中位数分数

\[s_{\mathrm{policy}}=\operatorname{median}_b x_b\]

Each round supplies the policy with a block-median score recomputed by the verifier from raw pairs. Externally supplied totals are not accepted. The final assessment uses a different aggregation rule.

每轮由核验者从原始配对数据重算块中位数，作为交给策略的分数，不接受外部填写的总分。最终评估另用一套聚合规则。

State change:状态变化： Verified policy observations and receipts updated; sizes not mixed.更新已验证的策略观测与回执；尺寸之间不混。

L06

An independent verified observation → posterior in the same space一条独立且已核验的观测 → 同空间后验

\[L_t(w)=P(o_t\mid h_f(w),a_t)\]
\[p_{t+1}(w)=\frac{p_t(w)\,L_t(w)}{Z_t}\]
derivation推导
\[Z_t=\sum_{v\in\Omega_s}p_t(v)L_t(v)\]
\[\ell_{t+1}(w)=\ell_t(w)+\ln L_t(w)-\ln Z_t\]

For each candidate world $w$, multiply its weight by the fixed likelihood of the outcome under its assigned factor semantics $h_f(w)$, then normalise. Recompute marginals from the joint distribution so dependencies are retained. A likelihood ratio of one leaves the weights unchanged but still marks the observation as used.

对每个候选世界 $w$，按其因子语义 $h_f(w)$ 下观测结果的固定似然加权，再归一化。边际分布从联合分布重新计算，以保留依赖关系。似然比为一时权重不变，但仍将观测记为已使用。

State change:状态变化： Updates the posterior version and used-evidence set; the graph can remain unchanged.更新后验版本与已使用证据集合，工作图可以保持不变。

P10

Posterior update, new snapshot后验更新，新快照

\[\widetilde p_{t+1}(w)=p_t(w)\,L_a\!\left(y\mid h_a(w)\right),\qquad p_{t+1}(w)=\frac{\widetilde p_{t+1}(w)}{\sum_v\widetilde p_{t+1}(v)}\]

Apply the reading loop's joint update, weighting each world by the outcome likelihood under its factor semantics. Preserve correlations with other factors and incorporate each observation only once.

使用与文献阅读循环相同的联合更新，根据各世界的因子语义和结果似然加权，保留与其他因子的相关性，并确保每份观测只纳入一次。

State change:状态变化： New posterior version; the graph is not changed by a score.新的后验版本；图不因分数而改变。

B07

Verified evidence: discrete outcome, likelihood, posterior已验证证据：离散结果、似然、后验

\[y=(z_p,z_s),\qquad \widetilde p_{t+1}(w)=p_t(w)\,L_a\!\left(y\mid h_a(w)\right),\qquad p_{t+1}(w)=\frac{\widetilde p_{t+1}(w)}{\sum_v\widetilde p_{t+1}(v)}\]

As in the probe loop, a verified trial supplies one discrete joint observation and its likelihood for updating. A failed trial supplies no posterior update.

与探针循环一致，经核验的试验提供一份离散联合观测及其更新似然，失败试验不更新后验。

State change:状态变化： Records one acquisition and update for each verified trial.每次通过核验的试验记录一次取材和更新。

L07

Updated joint belief → adopt a working graph?更新后的联合信念 → 是否采用工作图

\[\pi_t(h)=\sum_{w:\,h_f(w)=h}p_t(w)\]
\[h_t^\star=\underset{h\in\mathcal H_t^{\mathrm{alt}}}{\arg\max}\,\pi_t(h),\qquad \pi_t(h_t^\star)\ge\tau_{\mathrm{accept}}\]

Adoption is a decision separate from posterior updating. Select the alternative with the largest marginal and compare it with a predeclared research-policy threshold. This is not a significance test. The check is reached only after evidence was applied and the proposed patch passed validity checks.

工作图采用与后验更新是不同的决定。选择边际概率最大的备选，与预先规定的研究策略阈值比较；这不是显著性检验。只有本轮确实纳入证据、且补丁通过合法性检查，才进行采用判断。

State change:状态变化： Emits an adoption receipt; on failure the graph is unchanged.产生采用回执；未通过时图不变。

L08

Adopted structure → new working graph, successor actions采用的结构 → 新工作图与后继动作

\[G^+=\operatorname{Apply}(G^-,\Delta_h)\]
\[p^+(w)=p^-(w)\]

Adopting a candidate rebuilds the working graph and its successor actions while retaining joint weights and all alternative semantics. Changing the working representation adds no evidence and must preserve the candidate's scientific identity.

采用候选后，重建工作图及其后继动作，同时保留联合权重和所有备选语义。更换工作表示不增加证据，也不能改变候选所代表的科学含义。

State change:状态变化： Graph version and successor bindings change; the posterior does not.图版本和后继绑定改变；后验不变。

L10

A sourced local proposal → model admission有来源的局部提案 → 模型准入

\[\sigma_t=(S_t^{\mathrm{read}},g_t^{\mathrm{target}})\]
\[\delta_t^{\mathrm{norm}}=\operatorname{Normalize}(\delta_t^{\mathrm{raw}};\sigma_t),\qquad r_t^{\mathrm{adm}}=\operatorname{Admit}(\delta_t^{\mathrm{norm}},\mathcal B_t)\]
\[h_{\mathrm{new}}=\operatorname{Register}(\delta_t^{\mathrm{norm}},r_t^{\mathrm{adm}})\]

A proposal introduces a structure absent from the candidate space. Reconcile it with the read and target versions, then bind it to its original text span and scope before registering a new factor. The proposal itself is not confirming evidence.

提案描述现有空间中没有的结构。先对齐读取版本与目标版本，再绑定原文片段和适用范围，之后才能登记新因子。提出假设本身不构成支持该假设的证据。

State change:状态变化： Registers a candidate and its literature action; no evidence yet.登记候选及其文献动作；尚无证据。

L11

New candidate → new model space with a common prior新增候选 → 新模型空间与共同先验

\[M_s\longrightarrow M_{s+1}\]
\[p_0^{(s+1)}(w)=\frac{1}{\lvert\Omega^{(s+1)}_{\mathrm{legal}}\rvert},\qquad A_s=\operatorname{Archive}\!\left(p_t^{(s)}\right)\]

Enumerate the bounded set of legal semantic combinations, assign a fixed uniform prior and archive the old posterior. The old posterior is not multiplied into the new prior, and no cross-space posterior transport is assumed. Entropy and divergence before and after expansion are not directly comparable.

枚举有限范围内的合法语义组合，赋予固定均匀先验，并归档旧后验。旧后验不乘入新先验，也不假设可以跨空间搬运后验。扩展前后的熵与散度不能直接比较。

State change:状态变化： Creates a new space and world set, archives the old posterior and retains used-evidence identities to prevent duplicate confirmation.建立新空间及世界集合，归档旧后验，并保留已使用证据标识，防止重复确认。

L13

Budget out, no action, or a fault → sealed audit预算耗尽、无动作或故障 → 审计封存

\[\mathsf{Final}_{\mathrm{replay}}=\mathsf{Final}_{\mathrm{sealed}},\qquad\Delta N^{\mathrm{replay}}_{\mathrm{external}}=0\]

Record budget exhaustion, absence of scientific actions and engineering faults as distinct stopping reasons. Because attempts are persisted before external execution, an incomplete round without a valid receipt stops the loop. Replaying the sealed record reproduces the final state without launching new work.

分别记录预算耗尽、无科学动作可选和工程故障三种停止原因。由于尝试在外部执行前已保存，缺少有效回执的中断轮次会使循环停止。重放封存记录只复现终态，不启动新工作。

State change:状态变化： Final state or integrity audit saved; never overwritten.保存终态或完整性审计；从不覆写。

P12

Seal the experiment series, checkpoint failures and record results封存系列实验，保存失败检查点与结果

\[\operatorname{stop}\Leftarrow(N_t\ge B)\ \lor\ F_t\ \lor\ \mathcal A^{\mathrm{legal}}_t=\varnothing\ \lor\ J_t\le\epsilon\]
\[\operatorname{replay}(S,U)=(S,U)\]
derivation推导
\[J_t=\max_{a\in\mathcal A^{\mathrm{legal}}_t}\frac{I_t(a)}{c_a}\]

Distinguish four stopping reasons: spent budget, a mandatory-stop failure, no legal actions, or no actions with positive information value. Replay requires agreement between originals, modelling events and the policy ledger. Corrupt records are rejected without running replacement experiments.

区分四种停止原因：预算耗尽、必须停止的失败、无合法动作，以及无信息价值为正的动作。重放要求原件、建模事件和策略账本一致；记录损坏时拒绝重放，不通过补跑实验修补。

State change:状态变化： Completion, failure and recoverable interruption are different end states.完成、失败与可恢复中断是不同的终态。

B09

Seal the selection before the private reveal先封存选择，再揭示私有条件

\[\chi=(c^{*},h_s,h_c,\mathcal R_{\mathrm{pub}},h_\Pi,h_v),\qquad \operatorname{seal}(\chi)\prec\operatorname{reveal}(v),\qquad B_{\mathrm{private}}=1\]

Seal the candidate, source and configuration digests, public receipts, protocol digest and private commitment. Then reveal the private condition and allow one confirmation trial. Failure does not permit switching candidates and retrying.

共同封存候选、源码和配置摘要、公开回执、协议摘要与私有承诺。之后揭示私有条件，只允许一次确认试验；失败后不能更换候选重试。

State change:状态变化： Phase public → confirmation; the selection is unique and immutable.阶段 公开 → 确认；选择唯一且不可改。

B11

Assessment: public score, area under the curve, confirmation评估：公开分数、曲线下面积、确认分数

\[S_j(c)=\frac{1}{\lvert\mathcal Z_j\rvert}\sum_{z\in\mathcal Z_j}s_j(c,z),\qquad \operatorname{AUC}=\frac{1}{B_{\mathrm{pub}}}\sum_{k=1}^{B_{\mathrm{pub}}}I_k\]
derivation推导
\[s_j(c,z)=\frac1m\sum_{b=1}^{m}x_{j,c,z,b},\qquad M_{j,k}=\max\!\left(\{0\}\cup\mathcal S_{j,k}\right),\qquad I_k=\frac1J\sum_{j=1}^{J}M_{j,k}\]

Compute block means, then average equally across the full set of public sizes. Candidates with incomplete coverage contribute no score; the baseline contributes zero. At each paid attempt, record the best complete score reached so far. Failed attempts therefore reduce area under this progress curve.

先计算块均值，再对完整公开尺寸集等权平均。覆盖不完整的候选不计分，基线记零。每次付费尝试记录当时达到的最好完整分数，因此失败会降低进展曲线下面积。

State change:状态变化： Assessment only; it chooses no action and modifies no state.只做评估；不选动作，不改状态。

B12

Assess engineering completeness and scientific results分别评估工程完整性与科学结果

\[E=\mathbf 1\{\boldsymbol n=\boldsymbol B\}\cdot\mathbf 1\{C_{\mathrm{all}}\}\cdot\mathbf 1\{q_v=1\}\]
\[g_r=\mathbf 1\{S_r>0\},\qquad q_v=1:\ V_{+}=g_{\mathrm{pub}}\,g_{\mathrm{private}}\]

Engineering completeness $E$ requires all budgeted attempts to run correctly and a valid confirmation score. Only then compare public and private scores. $V_+$ denotes observed improvement, without a claim of statistical significance. Without valid confirmation, the result remains unconfirmed.

工程完整性 $E$ 要求所有预算内尝试正确执行，且确认分数有效，之后才能比较公开与私有分数。$V_+$ 仅表示观察到改善，不表示统计显著。缺少有效确认时，结果保留为未确认。

State change:状态变化： The verifier issues the verdict; afterwards only the originals can be audited.核验者给出裁决；此后只能审计原件。

1Interpreting the measurements怎样解读这些数值

These three quantities are defined within the model. Their interpretation needs the following limits.

以下三个量都有明确的模型内定义，使用时也需要注意其适用范围。

Expected information gain per cost is a pre-reading prediction单位成本的期望信息增益是阅读前的预测

\[\bar H(a)=\mathbb E_o\!\left[H(T\mid D,o,a)\right],\qquad \mathrm{EIG}(a)=H(T\mid D)-\bar H(a),\qquad s(a)=\frac{\mathrm{EIG}(a)}{c(a)}\]

The expected gain is calculated using the fixed likelihood table and declared cost. It ranks actions within that model; it does not measure model accuracy or the scientific value actually obtained.

期望增益依据固定似然表和声明成本计算，用于模型内的动作排序。它不能直接衡量模型是否符合真实世界，也不能代表实际获得的科学价值。

Entropy change and divergence only make sense inside one space熵变与散度只在同一空间内有意义

\[H(p)=-\sum_z p(z)\ln p(z),\qquad \Delta H=H(p_-)-H(p_+),\qquad \mathrm{KL}(p_+\Vert p_-)=\sum_z p_+(z)\ln\frac{p_+(z)}{p_-(z)}\]

Entropy change and divergence describe a belief update within the same candidate space. They are not comparable across an expansion of that space. A large divergence does not establish greater accuracy, and an individual observation can increase entropy.

熵变与散度描述同一候选空间内的信念更新，不能跨空间扩展直接比较。散度较大不说明判断更准确，单次观测也可能使熵增加。

Understanding still needs an external test理解仍需要外部检验

\[\mathrm{LogLoss}=-\ln p_y,\qquad \mathrm{Brier}=\sum_{k=1}^{K}\left(p_k-\mathbf 1_{\{y=k\}}\right)^2\]

To test whether predictive understanding improved, evaluate the posterior on held-out outcomes using strictly proper scoring rules. This is an external assessment of the loop, separate from its action-selection and update rules.

要检验预测性的理解是否改善，需要用严格正确的评分规则评估后验对留出结果的预测。这是对循环的外部检验，与循环内部的动作选择和更新规则分开。

Where the pieces come from这些部件的出处

  • Toth, Lorch et al., Active Bayesian Causal Inference, NeurIPS 2022, selects experiments by expected information gain about a causal query and updates after observing results. Here that process uses a finite discrete candidate space and fixed likelihood tables in place of a Gaussian-process or DiBS model.Toth、Lorch 等的 Active Bayesian Causal Inference（NeurIPS 2022）围绕因果问题按期望信息增益选择实验，再根据观测更新。这里沿用这一过程，以有限离散候选空间和固定似然表代替高斯过程或 DiBS 模型。
  • Gneiting & Raftery, Strictly Proper Scoring Rules, Prediction, and Estimation, JASA 2007: the basis for log loss and Brier as external tests of predictive understanding.Gneiting 与 Raftery，Strictly Proper Scoring Rules, Prediction, and Estimation，JASA 2007：log loss 和 Brier 作为预测性理解外部检验的依据。
  • Gao et al., ALCE, 2023: citation support and completeness as a way to check that what a reader extracted is actually in the source.Gao 等，ALCE，2023：引用支持性与完整性，用于检查阅读者提取的内容确实在原文中。

Writing down the steps is only part of the work. I still want to learn a policy that can choose when to read, when to measure, when to act on a belief and when to expand its hypotheses. The formal model makes those choices explicit; obtaining diverse training trajectories for them remains the costly part.

写出步骤只是这项工作的一部分。我还希望学到一个策略，能够决定何时阅读、何时测量、何时根据已有判断行动，以及何时扩展假设。形式化模型把这些选择明确下来，而获取足够多样的训练轨迹仍然成本很高。
