Can We Imitate the Steps by Which Humans Understand the World?我们能模仿人理解世界的步骤吗？

2026-09-08auto-researchbayesianactive-learningformal-model

What formal model does auto-research need?什么才是自动研究需要的形式化模型

A person who understands something can say what they believe, how sure they are, and what would change their mind. So the first thing an auto-research agent needs is not a longer context window but an explicit state: a working causal graph $G_t$ of the mechanism it currently adopts, and a joint posterior $p_t(w)$ over a frozen, finite space $\Omega_s$ of candidate worlds. Understanding lives in that pair, and only that pair is allowed to change when evidence arrives.

一个真正理解了某件事的人，说得出自己相信什么、有多确定、什么证据会让自己改主意。所以自动研究的 agent 首先需要的不是更长的上下文，而是一个显式的状态：一张它当前采用的工作因果图 $G_t$，以及一个冻结的有限候选世界空间 $\Omega_s$ 上的联合后验 $p_t(w)$。理解就存放在这一对对象里，证据到来时也只允许改这一对对象。

The second thing is that every way of touching the world, reading a paper, running a small probe, scoring on a benchmark, is the same kind of object: an action $a$ with a frozen observation model $L_a(o\mid w)$ and a declared cost $c(a)$. Then "what should I do next?" has one answer everywhere: the action with the largest expected information gain per unit cost about the question $T$ that matters now. Reading and experimenting differ only in the observation channel.

其次，接触世界的每一种方式，读一篇文献、跑一个小探针、在 benchmark 上打分，都应该是同一类对象：一个动作 $a$，自带冻结的观察模型 $L_a(o\mid w)$ 和声明成本 $c(a)$。这样"下一步做什么"在任何地方都只有一个答案：对当前关心的问题 $T$，单位成本期望信息增益最大的那个动作。读文献和做实验的区别只在观察通道。

The third thing is discipline about what counts. Only verified, independent observations enter the posterior, by Bayes and nothing else. Adopting a new working graph is a separate gate with its own threshold. Meeting a mechanism that is not in the candidate space is a third, explicit branch: extend the space, rebuild a common prior, archive the old posterior. And when the loop stops, the final state is sealed so that replaying the record changes nothing. These separations are what make the trace auditable, and auditability is what lets a person trust a machine's understanding.

第三是对"什么算数"的纪律。只有经过核验、相互独立的观测才能进入后验，而且只能经由 Bayes。采用一张新的工作图是另一道门，有自己的阈值。遇到候选空间里没有的机制是第三条显式支路：扩展空间、重建共同先验、归档旧后验。循环停下时终态被封存，重放记录不改变任何结果。正是这些分离让整条轨迹可审计，而可审计才是人能够信任机器理解的前提。

My take我的看法

Today's LLM agent frameworks have no explicit trade-off between exploring and executing, and no notion of first building an understanding and then deriving the best plan from it. A model that answers well is not the same as a model that knows what it does not know and spends its next hour accordingly.

目前的 LLM 框架缺少对探索和执行的权衡，也缺少"先显式构建理解、再基于理解产出最优方案"的框架。答得好的模型，和知道自己不知道什么、并据此安排下一小时的模型，不是一回事。

I think the reason is data, not architecture. This meta-behaviour is expensive to learn: exploration itself is costly, but the deeper cost is that an understanding of when to explore, when to commit and when to widen the hypothesis space can only be built from a large and diverse population of environments and trajectories. No single benchmark supplies it. The formal skeleton below is my attempt to write down what such a loop must look like, so that when the data exists we know what to train.

我认为原因在数据而不在架构。这种数据非常昂贵：不仅仅是探索昂贵，而是构建这种 meta 行为的理解，何时探索、何时执行、何时扩大假设空间，必须建立在大范围、多样的环境和轨迹之上。没有哪一个 benchmark 能单独提供它。下面的形式化骨架是我把这样一条循环写清楚的尝试：等数据存在时，我们知道该训练什么。

0 · One loop0 · 一条循环1 · Boundaries1 · 边界

0One loop, three observation channels一条循环，三种观察通道

Everything below is one abstract loop instantiated three times. The state is $S_t=(G_t,p_t)$; a round picks an action, obtains a verified observation, updates the posterior, decides whether to adopt a graph, and either continues, widens the candidate space, or stops and seals. The diagram is drawn from the formulas, not from an implementation.

下面的全部内容是同一条抽象循环的三次实例化。状态是 $S_t=(G_t,p_t)$；一轮里选一个动作、取得一份经核验的观测、更新后验、决定是否采用新图，然后要么续轮，要么扩展候选空间，要么停机封存。这张图由公式画出，不来自任何实现。

box方块start起点state状态choose选动作act & observe执行并观测verify核验update更新adopt a graph?采用新图？working graph工作图extend the space扩展候选空间stop & seal停机封存

Within a frozen candidate space, choose the literature action by expected information gain, turn a verified observation into a likelihood, update the joint posterior. A sourced proposal for a new mechanism takes the extension branch instead.

在冻结的候选空间内，用期望信息增益选择文献动作；把合格的观测变成似然，更新联合后验。发现新机制的提案另走扩展支路。

Import the understanding the reading loop produced, register one intervention with its observation model, calibrate the instrument, then let information gain choose what to measure and let the measurement update the same posterior.

导入读文献循环产出的理解，登记一个干预及其观察模型，先校准仪器，再让信息增益决定测什么，让测量结果更新同一个后验。

Public rounds learn the posterior and pick a candidate; the candidate is sealed before a private condition is revealed; the final score is recomputed from raw pairs. Belief feedback, performance history and the final verdict each keep their own arithmetic.

公开轮次学习后验并挑选候选；候选先封存，再揭示私有条件；最终分数从原始配对数据重算。信念反馈、性能历史和最终裁决各有各的算术。

P01

Upstream snapshot: scope, factor lineage, joint posterior上游快照：范围、因子谱系、联合后验

\[S_0=(G_0,p_0(W),\mathcal H_0,\mathcal L_0)\]
\[p_{\mathrm{import}}(W)=p_0(W),\qquad\mathcal R_{\mathrm{import}}=\mathcal R_0\]

The probe loop starts from the state the reading loop left: the graph, the joint posterior over worlds $W$, the candidate set and the ledger. The import keeps the posterior and the set of already-consumed observations $\mathcal R$; it does not reset to a uniform prior, and it only accepts mechanisms whose scope and semantics match.

探针循环从读文献循环留下的状态出发：图、世界 $W$ 上的联合后验、候选集合和账本。导入保留后验和已消费观测集合 $\mathcal R$，不重置为均匀先验，且只接受范围与语义匹配的机制。

State change:状态变化： Read-only upstream; a new generation with full history.上游只读；带完整历史的新一代状态。

B01

Fix the task, initialise the design model, or import a compatible one确定任务，初始化设计模型，或导入兼容模型

\[S_0=\operatorname{Init}(\tau,\sigma,\mathcal H_0)\quad\text{or}\quad p_{\mathrm{import}}(W)=p_{\mathrm{upstream}}(W)\]

A task $\tau$, a scope $\sigma$ and an initial hypothesis set $\mathcal H_0$ marked as design assumptions. Importing an upstream posterior is allowed only when scope and mechanism semantics match; models are never plugged in by node count.

任务 $\tau$、范围 $\sigma$、以及标注为设计假设的初始假设集 $\mathcal H_0$。只有范围与机制语义匹配时才允许导入上游后验；从不按节点数量接入模型。

State change:状态变化： An independent state, or an import that keeps its posterior.独立状态，或保留后验的导入。

B02

Freeze the protocol and commit to the private condition冻结协议，承诺私有条件

\[\Theta=(\tau,z,K,F_0,\mathcal C),\qquad \mathcal M=(\mathcal S,\mathcal O,\mu,B)\]
\[\Pi=(\Theta,\mathcal M,h_v),\qquad h_v=\operatorname{Hash}(v,\mathrm{salt}),\qquad \operatorname{freeze}(\Pi)\prec\operatorname{trial}_1\]

Everything that could be tuned after seeing results is frozen before the first trial: sizes, iterations, baseline $F_0$, candidate set, seed plan, pairing order, scoring rule $\mu$, budget $B$. The private condition $v$ is committed by a salted hash so it can be revealed later but not chosen later.

所有看到结果之后可能被调的东西都在第一次试验前冻结：尺寸、迭代数、基线 $F_0$、候选集、种子计划、配对顺序、评分规则 $\mu$、预算 $B$。私有条件 $v$ 以加盐哈希承诺，之后可以揭示，但不能事后选择。

State change:状态变化： Protocol frozen; private material isolated from the public policy.协议冻结；私有材料与公开策略隔离。

L09

Posterior updated, graph kept → next round后验已更新、图不改 → 下一轮

\[G_{t+1}=G_t,\qquad S_{t+1}=\operatorname{Snapshot}(G_{t+1},p_{t+1})\]
\[a_{t+1}^\star=\operatorname{Select}(S_{t+1})\]

Not crossing the adoption gate is not the same as not learning: the new weights feed the next information-gain computation. Evidence count, posterior version and graph version are three different counters. The next action may repeat the same question.

没跨过采用门不等于没学到东西：新权重进入下一次信息增益计算。证据数、后验版本、图版本是三个不同的计数。下一个动作可以继续问同一个问题。

State change:状态变化： Graph kept; the snapshot the policy sees is refreshed.图保持；策略所见的快照被刷新。

L12

A legal empty result → honest bookkeeping, next round合法的空结果 → 如实记账，续轮

\[\mathcal E_t^{\mathrm{verified}}=\varnothing\ \Longrightarrow\ p_{t+1}=p_t,\quad G_{t+1}=G_t\]

A complete delivery may carry no supporting observation; an observation whose independence cannot be established yields no likelihood. Neither is a refutation. The attempt, the acquisition and the processed result are still recorded: papers read is not the same count as informative evidence.

完整交付可以没有任何支持性观测；独立性无法建立的观测不产生似然。两者都不是反驳。尝试、取材和处理结果仍然入账：读过的篇数和信息性证据数是两个数。

State change:状态变化： Costs and acquisitions recorded; no fabricated consumption.记录成本与取材；没有虚构的消费。

P11

Optional commit receipt, next policy可选的采用回执，下一策略

\[S_{t+1}\longrightarrow a_{t+1}\longrightarrow\mathcal C_{t+1},\qquad m_G=0\Longrightarrow G_{t+1}=G_t\]

The next policy carries the new direction, its observation binding and the catalogue. A flag $m_G$ says whether this campaign may mutate the formal graph at all; with it off, belief moves and the graph stays. History migrates between rounds only when semantics and compatibility hashes all match.

下一策略携带新的方向、观察绑定和目录。标志 $m_G$ 决定本次 campaign 是否允许改动正式图；关闭时信念移动、图不动。历史只有在语义和兼容哈希全部匹配时才跨轮迁移。

State change:状态变化： Information gain, catalogue and next size refreshed.刷新信息增益、目录与下一尺寸。

B08

Next policy: new action binding, next frozen size下一策略：新的动作绑定、下一冻结尺寸

\[(S_{t+1},\mathcal C_t,U_t)\longrightarrow(d_{t+1},\mathcal C_{t+1},U_{t+1})\]
\[\operatorname{alias}(c,\widehat c)\Longrightarrow\kappa(c)=\kappa(\widehat c)\]

The next policy binds the new direction, catalogue and size. History is keyed by $\kappa$, a semantic identity plus compatibility hashes; two candidates share history only when the keys are equal, and ambiguity is refused. Different sizes are never pooled as repeats of one experiment.

下一策略绑定新的方向、目录和尺寸。历史以 $\kappa$ 为键，即语义身份加兼容哈希；两个候选只有键相等时才共享历史，有歧义则拒绝。不同尺寸从不合并成同一实验的重复。

State change:状态变化： The dynamic loop closes; the next trial consumes the full next policy.动态闭环；下一次试验消费完整的下一策略。

L01

Current posterior → the confirming action当前后验 → 确认取证动作

\[\mathrm{EIG}_t(a)=H_t(T)-\bar H_t(a)\]
\[a_t^\star=\underset{a\in\mathcal A_{\mathrm{lit}}(M_s)}{\arg\max}\frac{\mathrm{EIG}_t(a)}{c(a)}\]
derivation推导
\[p_t(o\mid a)=\sum_{w\in\Omega_s}p_t(w)L_a(o\mid w)\]
\[\bar H_t(a)=\sum_{o\in\mathcal O_a}p_t(o\mid a)H_t^{a,o}(T)\]

Project the joint posterior onto the factor the question $T$ is about, predict each action's outcome distribution through its frozen likelihood table, and rank by expected entropy drop per declared cost. The score must be strictly positive. It is a prediction made before reading, not a measure of understanding.

把联合后验投影到问题 $T$ 涉及的因子上，用每个动作冻结的似然表预测其结果分布，按单位声明成本的期望熵下降排序，分数必须严格为正。它是阅读前的预测，不是理解质量的度量。

State change:状态变化： Selects and binds an action; the posterior and the graph do not change.只选择并绑定动作；后验与工作图不变。

L02

Current graph and its last change → a discovery question当前工作图与最近一次改动 → 发现问题

\[\phi_t=\operatorname{Focus}(G_t,\Delta G_{\mathrm{last}})\]
\[Q_t^{\mathrm{disc}}=\operatorname{Request}(\phi_t)\]

A bounded, deterministic focus: prefer the nodes most recently added to the working graph and their downstream links, else the changed edges, else the original intent. At most one sourced local proposal may come back. No information gain is computed for hypotheses that do not exist yet.

有界、确定性的选焦点：优先取最近加入工作图的节点及其下游连线，否则取变更的边，再否则回到初始意图。最多允许返回一个有来源的局部提案。不为尚未存在的假设计算信息增益。

State change:状态变化： Sets this round's discovery focus; adds no mechanism.设定本轮的发现焦点；不新增机制。

P02

Register the experiment action: intervention, observation model, scope登记实验动作：干预、观察模型、范围

\[a=(\operatorname{do}(f),\,q,\,L_a,\,\sigma,\,c_a)\]
\[p_{\mathrm{registered}}(W)=p_0(W)\]

An experiment is an action like any other: an intervention $\operatorname{do}(f)$, the question $q$ it informs, a frozen observation model $L_a$, an applicability scope $\sigma$ and a declared cost. Registering it creates an identity, not support.

实验和别的动作是同一类对象：一个干预 $\operatorname{do}(f)$、它所回答的问题 $q$、冻结的观察模型 $L_a$、适用范围 $\sigma$ 和声明成本。登记只创建身份，不产生支持。

State change:状态变化： New action identity; posterior and old observations unchanged.新的动作身份；后验与旧观测不变。

P04

Direction: target entropy, information gain, cost方向：目标熵、信息增益、成本

\[I_t(a)=H_t-\sum_y p_t^a(y)\,H_t^a(y)\]
\[a_t\in\underset{a\in\mathcal A^{\mathrm{legal}}_t}{\arg\max}\ \frac{I_t(a)}{c_a}\]
derivation推导
\[H_t=H(q\mid D_t),\qquad H_t^a(y)=H(q\mid D_t,y,a)\]

The same rule as in reading: the modelling step computes, from the current posterior and the frozen observation model, how much each legal action is expected to reduce the entropy of the question. The policy consumes these numbers with the action hash attached; it cannot invent an information score.

与读文献同一条规则：建模步骤根据当前后验和冻结的观察模型，算出每个合法动作预期能把问题的熵降低多少。策略消费这些带动作哈希的数值，不能自己编一个信息分。

State change:状态变化： Produces a direction; the posterior is untouched.产出方向；后验不动。

P05

Catalogue bound to the direction, frozen size schedule绑定方向的候选目录与冻结尺寸序列

\[\mathcal C_t=\operatorname{Catalog}(S_t,a_t,\mathcal M,z_t),\qquad z_t\in\mathcal Z=\{z_1,\dots,z_K\}\]

The executable catalogue is rebuilt each round from the current snapshot, the chosen action, the frozen method set $\mathcal M$ and the size scheduled for this round. One intervention at several sizes is one experiment repeated, not several methods.

可执行目录每轮从当前快照、选定动作、冻结方法集 $\mathcal M$ 和本轮排定的尺寸重建。一个干预在多个尺寸上是同一实验的重复，不是多种方法。

State change:状态变化： Catalogue bound to the snapshot; per-size histories kept apart.目录绑定快照；各尺寸历史分开保存。

B03

Snapshot, direction, mechanism action快照、方向、机制动作

\[d_t=f\!\left(G_t,\,p_t(W),\,\mathcal A^{\mathrm{legal}}_t\right),\qquad \mathcal C_t=\operatorname{Catalog}(d_t,\mathcal M,z_t)\]

Each round rebuilds the direction from the current graph, posterior and legal actions, and rebuilds the catalogue against it. The modelling identity is refreshed every round rather than frozen through the campaign; lineage of semantics is preserved.

每轮从当前图、后验和合法动作重建方向，并据此重建目录。建模身份每轮刷新，而不是整个 campaign 冻结一次；语义谱系被保留。

State change:状态变化： Direction and candidate identities updated.方向与候选身份更新。

B04

Performance exploration: unmeasured first, then greedy on verified means性能探索：先测未测的，再按已验证均值贪心

\[\mathcal C^0_t\ne\varnothing\Longrightarrow c_t\in\mathcal C^0_t\]
\[\mathcal C^0_t=\varnothing\Longrightarrow c_t\in\underset{c\in\mathcal C_t}{\arg\max}\ \overline u_t(c)\ \ (\text{w.p. }1-\varepsilon)\]
derivation推导
\[\overline u_t(c)=\frac{1}{n_t(c)}\sum_{i=1}^{n_t(c)}u_i(c)\]

The performance objective uses a deliberately simple policy: measure every legal candidate once in a deterministic order, then epsilon-greedy on the mean of verified scores from compatible history. Nothing is optimised by a surrogate; nothing is generated.

性能目标用一个刻意简单的策略：按确定顺序把每个合法候选先测一次，然后对兼容历史中已验证分数的均值做 epsilon-greedy。没有代理模型优化，也不生成新候选。

State change:状态变化： A measurement slot is reserved; a failure still counts.预约一个测量位；失败同样计数。

L03

Questions and scope → retrieval, full text frozen问题与范围 → 检索，全文冻结

\[\mathcal Q_t=\operatorname{dedup}(q_t^{\mathrm{conf}},q_t^{\mathrm{disc}}),\qquad 1\le\lvert\mathcal Q_t\rvert\le 2\]
\[F_t=\operatorname{Retrieve}(\mathcal Q_t,B_t)\]

The confirming query goes first, the discovery query is appended if a focus exists. The round's reservation and read version are persisted before any external call; retrieved originals, full text and custody are frozen. Nothing is conditioned on yet.

确认查询在前，有焦点时追加发现查询。调用外部之前先持久化本轮预约和读取版本；检索原件、全文和保管记录被冻结。这一步不对任何东西做条件化。

State change:状态变化： Adds retrieved material and source identities; consumed evidence unchanged.新增检索材料与来源身份；已消费证据不变。

L04

Frozen text and current graph → the reader冻结全文与当前图 → 阅读者

\[I_t=(G_t^{\mathrm{read}},\mathsf T_t,M_t,C_t,F_t)\]
\[\boldsymbol b_t=\operatorname{Hashes}(I_t)\]

The reader receives one frozen bundle: the graph version it reads against, its task, the source manifest, custody, and the text. Immutable hashes fix which material was read; they say nothing about causal truth. The reader may only write an extraction and a report, never a likelihood or a confidence.

阅读者收到一个冻结的输入包：它所对照的图版本、任务、来源清单、保管记录和全文。不可变哈希固定"读的是哪份材料"，不评价因果真伪。阅读者只能写提取结果和报告，不能写似然或可信度。

State change:状态变化： Produces extraction artefacts; cannot touch the posterior.产出提取工件；不能碰后验。

P03

Freeze the runtime, calibrate the instrument冻结运行环境，校准仪器

\[m_b=\operatorname{median}_i T_{b,i},\qquad d=\max_b\frac{\lvert m_{b+1}-m_b\rvert}{m_b}\]
\[g_{\mathrm{cal}}=\mathbf 1\{C\ \land\ d\le\delta_{\mathrm{cal}}\}\]

Before any trial, the baseline is timed in several blocks; the largest relative drift between consecutive block medians must stay under a frozen tolerance and every block must be correct. A failed calibration is kept as a receipt and blocks the loop; it is not evidence about the mechanism.

任何试验之前，先分几块给基线计时；相邻块中位数之间最大的相对漂移必须低于冻结的容差，且每块都正确。失败的校准作为回执保留并阻断循环；它不是关于机制的证据。

State change:状态变化： Consumes the calibration slot; ready only on pass; no posterior change.消耗校准位；通过才就绪；后验不变。

P06

Plan, and reserve the attempt before running计划，并在运行前预约尝试

\[N_t=\lvert\mathcal A_t\rvert,\qquad \operatorname{start}_{t+1}\Longrightarrow N_t<B\]

Budget is enforced on reserved attempts, not on successes: the reservation is persisted first, a failure still counts, and a replay adds nothing. Enough budget is necessary but not sufficient; readiness and execution guards must also pass.

预算按已预约的尝试计，而不是按成功计：预约先持久化，失败照样计数，重放不新增。预算充足是必要条件而非充分条件；就绪和执行守卫也必须通过。

State change:状态变化： Ledger reserved → started; the plan gets an immutable identity.账本 预约 → 启动；计划获得不可变身份。

P07

Raw evidence: paired timings, allocation trace, correctness原始证据：配对计时、分配轨迹、正确性

\[m_{a,b}=\operatorname{median}_i T_{a,b,i}\]
\[x_b=\ln\!\left(\frac{m_{\mathrm{base},b}}{m_{\mathrm{probe},b}}\right)\]

Baseline and probe are timed in interleaved paired blocks with the same seed; the per-block statistic is the log ratio of medians, in nats, positive when the probe is faster. Repeated timings inside a block are not independent experiments.

基线和探针以同一种子在交错的配对块中计时；每块的统计量是中位数的对数比，单位 nat，探针更快时为正。块内的重复计时不是独立实验。

State change:状态变化： Only raw evidence exists; execution success is not verification.只存在原始证据；执行成功不等于通过核验。

B05

Locked plan: one coordinate intervened, everything else equal锁定计划：只动一个坐标，其余全等

\[\theta_c=\theta_{F_0}\ \text{except}\ \operatorname{do}(\theta_k:\ \theta\to\theta^\prime)\]
\[x_b=\ln\!\left(\frac{m_{F_0,b}}{m_{c,b}}\right)\]

A candidate differs from the baseline in exactly one intervened coordinate; grid, steps, precision and inputs are identical and checked against a fixed reference. The per-block statistic is the same paired log-ratio as in the probe loop.

候选与基线只在一个被干预的坐标上不同；网格、步数、精度和输入完全相同，并对照固定参考校验。每块统计量与探针循环相同，是配对对数比。

State change:状态变化： Immutable raw data, environment and plan hash; no posterior write.不可变的原始数据、环境和计划哈希；不写后验。

B10

Private confirmation私有确认

\[g_v=I_{c^{*}}\land I_{z,\mathcal S}\land C\land D\]
\[s_v=\begin{cases}\dfrac1m\sum_{b=1}^{m}x_b,&g_v=1\\[6pt]\bot,&g_v=0\end{cases}\]

The confirmation score exists only if the candidate identity, the frozen private size and seeds, correctness and the drift gate all hold. Otherwise the score is $\bot$, which is not zero, and the budget slot is still consumed.

只有候选身份、冻结的私有尺寸与种子、正确性和漂移门全部成立，确认分数才存在。否则分数为 $\bot$，它不是零，而预算位照样消耗。

State change:状态变化： The last slot is spent; a failure gives no trusted update.最后一个预算位被消耗；失败不给出可信更新。

L05

Extraction and proposal → verified against the originals提取与提案 → 对照原件核验

\[R_t=(\mathcal O_t,\mathcal P_t,s_t),\qquad\lvert\mathcal P_t\rvert\le 1\]
\[V_t=\operatorname{Verify}(R_t,I_t,r_t)\]

Spans and hashes are checked against the original, then scope, source identity, coverage, action and model binding, and the invocation receipt. Each item is routed to one of: consumable evidence, model admission, inconclusive, invalid. A complete delivery with both sets empty is legal; a pending one is not a delivery.

先核对原文片段与哈希，再核对范围、来源身份、覆盖度、动作与模型绑定以及调用回执。每一项被分流到：可消费证据、模型准入、不确定、无效。两个集合都为空的完整交付是合法的；未完成的交付不算交付。

State change:状态变化： Routes raw output; failures and uncertainty never fabricate an update.分流原始输出；失败与不确定不会造出更新。

P08

Verification receipt and verified score核验回执与已验证分数

\[s_t=\operatorname{median}_b x_b\]
\[r_t\notin\mathcal R_t:\ U_{t+1}=U_t\cup\{(r_t,s_t)\},\qquad r_t\in\mathcal R_t:\ U_{t+1}=U_t\]

The verifier recomputes the score from the raw pairs and issues a receipt $r_t$. The policy's history $U$ only ever grows by receipts it has not seen; a conflicting receipt for a known identity is rejected. A score alone informs the policy, never the world model.

核验者从原始配对数据重算分数并签发回执 $r_t$。策略历史 $U$ 只因未见过的回执而增长；已知身份的冲突回执被拒绝。单独的分数只告知策略，从不告知世界模型。

State change:状态变化： Policy history updated; the modelling state is not.策略历史更新；建模状态不变。

P09

Verified evidence: one discrete joint outcome and its log-likelihood已验证证据：一个离散联合结果及其对数似然

\[y=(z_p,z_s),\qquad \ell_h=\ln L_a(y\mid h)\]
derivation推导
\[\overline B_a=\operatorname{median}_b B_{a,b},\qquad r=1-\frac{\overline B_{\mathrm{probe}}}{\overline B_{\mathrm{base}}}\]
\[z_p=\mathbf 1\{r\ge\rho\},\qquad z_s=\begin{cases}+,&s_t\ge\delta_s\\0,&-\delta_s<s_t<\delta_s\\-,&s_t\le-\delta_s\end{cases}\]

Proxy and speed are combined into one observation: the proxy reduction $r$ is thresholded at $\rho$, the speed score at $\pm\delta_s$. The likelihood table is a frozen design assumption that has not been empirically calibrated. If the proxy is missing or outside the table's support, only a score is produced, not a likelihood.

代理量和速度合成一份观测：代理减少量 $r$ 以 $\rho$ 为阈值，速度分数以 $\pm\delta_s$ 为阈值。似然表是冻结的设计假设，尚未经验校准。代理量缺失或超出表的支持范围时，只给分数，不给似然。

State change:状态变化： On pass, one acquisition is recorded and one likelihood absorbed.通过时记录一次取材、吸收一次似然。

B06

Verification receipt and verified score, per-round median核验回执与已验证分数，逐轮中位口径

\[s_{\mathrm{policy}}=\operatorname{median}_b x_b\]

The score handed to the policy each round is the block median, recomputed by the verifier from raw pairs; an externally supplied total is never trusted. It is deliberately a different aggregate from the final assessment.

每轮交给策略的分数是块中位数，由核验者从原始配对数据重算；外部填写的总分从不采信。它刻意与最终评估使用不同的聚合方式。

State change:状态变化： Verified policy observations and receipts updated; sizes not mixed.更新已验证的策略观测与回执；尺寸之间不混。

L06

An independent verified observation → posterior in the same space一条独立且已核验的观测 → 同空间后验

\[L_t(w)=P(o_t\mid h_f(w),a_t)\]
\[p_{t+1}(w)=\frac{p_t(w)\,L_t(w)}{Z_t}\]
derivation推导
\[Z_t=\sum_{v\in\Omega_s}p_t(v)L_t(v)\]
\[\ell_{t+1}(w)=\ell_t(w)+\ln L_t(w)-\ln Z_t\]

Bayes on the joint space: every candidate world $w$ is weighted by the frozen likelihood of the observed outcome under the semantics $h_f(w)$ it assigns to the tested factor, then normalised. Marginals are recomputed from the joint, never treated as independent. An observation with likelihood ratio one is recorded as consumed and leaves the numbers unchanged.

联合空间上的 Bayes：每个候选世界 $w$ 按其对被检验因子赋予的语义 $h_f(w)$ 下观测结果的冻结似然加权，再归一化。边际从联合分布重算，从不当作独立量。似然比为一的观测记为已消费，数值不变。

State change:状态变化： Posterior version and consumed set advance; the graph may stay.后验版本与已消费集合前进；工作图可以不变。

P10

Posterior update, new snapshot后验更新，新快照

\[\widetilde p_{t+1}(w)=p_t(w)\,L_a\!\left(y\mid h_a(w)\right),\qquad p_{t+1}(w)=\frac{\widetilde p_{t+1}(w)}{\sum_v\widetilde p_{t+1}(v)}\]

Exactly the reading-loop update on the full joint: each world is weighted by the likelihood of the outcome under the semantics it assigns to the tested factor. Correlations with other factors are preserved; a consumed observation is never counted twice.

与读文献循环完全相同的联合更新：每个世界按它对被检验因子赋予的语义下观测结果的似然加权。与其他因子的相关性被保留；已消费的观测从不计两次。

State change:状态变化： New posterior version; the graph is not changed by a score.新的后验版本；图不因分数而改变。

B07

Verified evidence: discrete outcome, likelihood, posterior已验证证据：离散结果、似然、后验

\[y=(z_p,z_s),\qquad \widetilde p_{t+1}(w)=p_t(w)\,L_a\!\left(y\mid h_a(w)\right),\qquad p_{t+1}(w)=\frac{\widetilde p_{t+1}(w)}{\sum_v\widetilde p_{t+1}(v)}\]

The same observation and the same update as in the probe loop: a verified trial becomes one discrete joint outcome and one likelihood; a failed trial produces no update at all.

与探针循环相同的观测和更新：一次经核验的试验变成一个离散联合结果和一个似然；失败的试验不产生任何更新。

State change:状态变化： One trial, one acquisition, one update.一次试验、一次取材、一次更新。

L07

Updated joint belief → adopt a working graph?更新后的联合信念 → 是否采用工作图

\[\pi_t(h)=\sum_{w:\,h_f(w)=h}p_t(w)\]
\[h_t^\star=\underset{h\in\mathcal H_t^{\mathrm{alt}}}{\arg\max}\,\pi_t(h),\qquad \pi_t(h_t^\star)\ge\tau_{\mathrm{accept}}\]

The posterior is evidence weight; adoption is an action gate. Among alternatives to the current baseline, take the one with the largest marginal and compare with a threshold that was frozen as research policy, not as statistical significance. The gate is only reachable in a round that actually applied evidence and whose patch is legal.

后验是证据权重，采用是行动门。在当前基线之外的备选中取边际最大者，与一个作为研究策略预先冻结的阈值比较，它不是统计显著性。只有确实应用了证据、且补丁合法的一轮才能走到这道门。

State change:状态变化： Emits an adoption receipt; on failure the graph is unchanged.产生采用回执；未通过时图不变。

L08

Adopted structure → new working graph, successor actions采用的结构 → 新工作图与后继动作

\[G^+=\operatorname{Apply}(G^-,\Delta_h)\]
\[p^+(w)=p^-(w)\]

Rebasing the working graph onto the adopted candidate rebuilds structure and successor actions while keeping the joint weights and all alternative semantics. Switching which graph is adopted adds no evidence and cannot change the scientific identity of the adopted candidate.

把工作图 rebase 到被采用的候选上，重建结构和后继动作，同时保留联合权重和所有备择语义。切换"采用哪张图"不增加证据，也不能改变被采用候选的科学身份。

State change:状态变化： Graph version and successor bindings change; the posterior does not.图版本和后继绑定改变；后验不变。

L10

A sourced local proposal → model admission有来源的局部提案 → 模型准入

\[\sigma_t=(S_t^{\mathrm{read}},g_t^{\mathrm{target}})\]
\[\delta_t^{\mathrm{norm}}=\operatorname{Normalize}(\delta_t^{\mathrm{raw}};\sigma_t),\qquad r_t^{\mathrm{adm}}=\operatorname{Admit}(\delta_t^{\mathrm{norm}},\mathcal B_t)\]
\[h_{\mathrm{new}}=\operatorname{Register}(\delta_t^{\mathrm{norm}},r_t^{\mathrm{adm}})\]

A proposal describes a candidate structure that is not in the space. It is normalised between the version it was read against and the current target, then bound to the original text span and scope before it can be registered as a new candidate factor. A proposal is never treated as its own confirmation.

提案描述一种空间里尚不存在的候选结构。它先在被读取的版本与当前目标版本之间归一化，再绑定到原文片段和范围，然后才能注册为新的候选因子。提案从不被当作对它自己的确认。

State change:状态变化： Registers a candidate and its literature action; no evidence yet.登记候选及其文献动作；尚无证据。

L11

New candidate → new model space with a common prior新增候选 → 新模型空间与共同先验

\[M_s\longrightarrow M_{s+1}\]
\[p_0^{(s+1)}(w)=\frac{1}{\lvert\Omega^{(s+1)}_{\mathrm{legal}}\rvert},\qquad A_s=\operatorname{Archive}\!\left(p_t^{(s)}\right)\]

Extension enumerates the bounded legal combinations of semantics, freezes a uniform prior over them and archives the old posterior. The old posterior is not multiplied into the new prior; posterior transport across spaces is not claimed. Entropy or divergence before and after extension are not comparable and are not a learning gain.

扩展枚举有界的合法语义组合，在其上冻结均匀先验，并归档旧后验。旧后验不乘进新先验；不宣称跨空间的后验搬运。扩展前后的熵或散度不可比，也不是学习收益。

State change:状态变化： New space identity and joint world set; old posterior archived; consumed identities kept so the same material cannot confirm twice.新的空间身份与联合世界集合；旧后验归档；保留已消费身份，同一份材料不能二次确认。

L13

Budget out, no action, or a fault → sealed audit预算耗尽、无动作或故障 → 审计封存

\[\mathsf{Final}_{\mathrm{replay}}=\mathsf{Final}_{\mathrm{sealed}},\qquad\Delta N^{\mathrm{replay}}_{\mathrm{external}}=0\]

The loop stops on exhausted budget, on the absence of any scientific action, or on an engineering fault, and the three are kept apart. Attempts are persisted before external work, so a partial round without a valid receipt stops closed rather than being repaired by guesswork. Replaying the sealed record reproduces the final state and starts nothing.

循环因预算耗尽、没有任何科学动作、或工程故障而停止，三者分开记录。尝试在外部执行之前已持久化，所以缺少有效回执的半途一轮会关闭停止，而不是靠猜测补齐。重放封存的记录只复现终态，不启动任何东西。

State change:状态变化： Final state or integrity audit saved; never overwritten.保存终态或完整性审计；从不覆写。

P12

Sealed campaign, failure checkpoint, final result封存的 campaign、失败检查点、最终结果

\[\operatorname{stop}\Leftarrow(N_t\ge B)\ \lor\ F_t\ \lor\ \mathcal A^{\mathrm{legal}}_t=\varnothing\ \lor\ J_t\le\epsilon\]
\[\operatorname{replay}(S,U)=(S,U)\]
derivation推导
\[J_t=\max_{a\in\mathcal A^{\mathrm{legal}}_t}\frac{I_t(a)}{c_a}\]

Four stop conditions, kept distinct: budget spent, a must-stop failure, no legal action, or no action with positive information value. Replay is only valid when originals, modelling events and policy ledger agree; a corrupted record is refused rather than repaired by new experiments.

四个停止条件分开记录：预算用尽、必须停止的失败、没有合法动作、没有信息价值为正的动作。只有原件、建模事件和策略账本一致时重放才有效；损坏的记录被拒绝，而不是用新实验修补。

State change:状态变化： Completion, failure and recoverable interruption are different end states.完成、失败与可恢复中断是不同的终态。

B09

Seal the selection before the private reveal先封存选择，再揭示私有条件

\[\chi=(c^{*},h_s,h_c,\mathcal R_{\mathrm{pub}},h_\Pi,h_v),\qquad \operatorname{seal}(\chi)\prec\operatorname{reveal}(v),\qquad B_{\mathrm{private}}=1\]

The selected candidate, source and config digests, every public receipt, the protocol digest and the private commitment are sealed together. Only then is the private condition revealed, and exactly one confirmation is allowed: a failed confirmation cannot be retried with another candidate.

被选候选、源码与配置摘要、全部公开回执、协议摘要和私有承诺一起封存。此后才揭示私有条件，且只允许一次确认：确认失败不能换候选重试。

State change:状态变化： Phase public → confirmation; the selection is unique and immutable.阶段 公开 → 确认；选择唯一且不可改。

B11

Assessment: public score, area under the curve, confirmation评估：公开分数、曲线下面积、确认分数

\[S_j(c)=\frac{1}{\lvert\mathcal Z_j\rvert}\sum_{z\in\mathcal Z_j}s_j(c,z),\qquad \operatorname{AUC}=\frac{1}{B_{\mathrm{pub}}}\sum_{k=1}^{B_{\mathrm{pub}}}I_k\]
derivation推导
\[s_j(c,z)=\frac1m\sum_{b=1}^{m}x_{j,c,z,b},\qquad M_{j,k}=\max\!\left(\{0\}\cup\mathcal S_{j,k}\right),\qquad I_k=\frac1J\sum_{j=1}^{J}M_{j,k}\]

The final assessment uses block means, then an equal-weight average over the complete set of public sizes; a candidate without complete coverage contributes nothing and the baseline contributes zero. The curve is the best complete score reached by each paid position, so failures cost area.

最终评估先取块均值，再对完整覆盖的公开尺寸集等权平均；覆盖不完整的候选不贡献，基线贡献为零。曲线是每个付费位置为止达到的最好完整分数，因此失败会损失面积。

State change:状态变化： Assessment only; it chooses no action and modifies no state.只做评估；不选动作，不改状态。

B12

Two-axis verdict: engineering and science两轴裁决：工程与科学

\[E=\mathbf 1\{\boldsymbol n=\boldsymbol B\}\cdot\mathbf 1\{C_{\mathrm{all}}\}\cdot\mathbf 1\{q_v=1\}\]
\[g_r=\mathbf 1\{S_r>0\},\qquad q_v=1:\ V_{+}=g_{\mathrm{pub}}\,g_{\mathrm{private}}\]

Engineering completeness $E$ asks whether every budgeted slot ran, every run was correct and the confirmation score is valid. Only then are public and private scores compared, and $V_+$ means an observed improvement, not a significant one. Without a valid confirmation the verdict stays at not confirmed: neither improved nor no-improvement.

工程完整性 $E$ 问：每个预算位都跑了、每次执行都正确、确认分数有效。只有这时才比较公开与私有分数，$V_+$ 表示观察到提高，不表示显著。没有有效确认时裁决停留在未确认：既不是已提高，也不是无改善。

State change:状态变化： The verifier issues the verdict; afterwards only the originals can be audited.核验者给出裁决；此后只能审计原件。

1What the numbers mean, and what they do not这些数值意味着什么，不意味着什么

Three quantities appear throughout. Each is well defined inside the model and each is easy to over-read.

有三个量贯穿全篇。每个在模型内都定义良好，每个都容易被过度解读。

Expected information gain per cost is a pre-reading prediction单位成本的期望信息增益是阅读前的预测

\[\bar H(a)=\mathbb E_o\!\left[H(T\mid D,o,a)\right],\qquad \mathrm{EIG}(a)=H(T\mid D)-\bar H(a),\qquad s(a)=\frac{\mathrm{EIG}(a)}{c(a)}\]

The expectation is taken under the frozen finite likelihood table and the cost is a declared value. The score ranks actions inside the model; it is not a measure of how well the model matches the world, nor of the scientific value actually obtained.

期望在冻结的有限似然表下取，成本是声明值。这个分数在模型内给动作排序；它不度量模型与世界的吻合程度，也不度量实际取得的科学价值。

Entropy change and divergence only make sense inside one space熵变与散度只在同一空间内有意义

\[H(p)=-\sum_z p(z)\ln p(z),\qquad \Delta H=H(p_-)-H(p_+),\qquad \mathrm{KL}(p_+\Vert p_-)=\sum_z p_+(z)\ln\frac{p_+(z)}{p_-(z)}\]

These describe how a belief moved after an update in the same candidate space. Across a model-space extension they are not comparable. A large divergence does not mean the belief is more correct, and a single observation need not lower entropy.

它们描述同一候选空间内一次更新后信念的移动。跨模型空间扩展时不可比。散度大不代表信念更正确，单条观测也不必然降低熵。

Understanding still needs an external test理解仍需要外部检验

\[\mathrm{LogLoss}=-\ln p_y,\qquad \mathrm{Brier}=\sum_{k=1}^{K}\left(p_k-\mathbf 1_{\{y=k\}}\right)^2\]

Whether the posterior predicts held-out outcomes better than before is the only thing that would show understanding improved. Strictly proper scoring rules are the right instrument for that, and this check sits outside the loop: it is what the loop should be judged by, not part of it.

后验对留出结果的预测是否比以前更好，是唯一能说明理解确有改进的东西。严格正确的评分规则是做这件事的正确工具，而这项检验在循环之外：它是评判循环的标准，不是循环的一部分。

So, can we imitate the steps? The steps can be written down; that part is done above. What is not done is a policy that follows them by itself: one that knows when reading beats measuring, when a belief is strong enough to act on, and when the honest move is to admit the hypothesis space was too small. That is a learning problem, and its data is the expensive part.

那么，我们能模仿这些步骤吗？步骤是写得出来的，上面已经写完。没做完的是一个能自己走完这些步骤的策略：知道什么时候读比测更值、什么时候信念强到可以行动、什么时候诚实的做法是承认假设空间太小。这是一个学习问题，而它的数据才是昂贵的部分。
