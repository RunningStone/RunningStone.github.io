How the costs were estimated成本怎么估的

Compute. For each method I take the reference run as published and count the tokens every resident model processes, by role: tokens that receive a gradient cost 6 FLOP per parameter; forward-only scoring (reference model, reward model, judge, teacher, classifier) costs 2; autoregressive generation costs 2 but runs memory-bound. Auxiliary models are scaled by the ratio of their active parameters to the student's, so everything is per parameter of the model being trained. Post-training methods count only their own step, never the base model. Pretraining methods count the run the recipe prescribes; data pipelines count their GPU scoring, not CPU filtering or deduplication. Where a paper reports GPU-hours (DeepSeek-V3, DeepSeek-R1) that number is used directly. Methods that train nothing on a GPU (prompting, rule verifiers, environment construction) sit at a floor and are flagged.

算力。每个方法取论文里的参考配方，按角色数每个驻留模型处理的 token：有梯度的 token 每参数 6 FLOP；只前向的打分（参照模型、奖励模型、裁判、教师、分类器）每参数 2 FLOP；自回归生成也是 2 FLOP 但受显存带宽限制。辅助模型按其激活参数与学生的比例折算，所以一切都是「每个被训练模型的参数」。后训练方法只算自己这一步，不含基座；预训练方法算该配方规定的整个 run；数据流水线只算 GPU 打分，不算 CPU 过滤与去重。论文报告了 GPU 小时的（DeepSeek-V3、DeepSeek-R1）直接采用。不在 GPU 上训练的方法（提示、规则验证器、搭环境）放在底线并标出。

Price. H100 SXM at $3.00/GPU-hour, the on-demand market median in September 2026 (aggregators quote about $3.4; marketplaces $2–3; hyperscalers $7–12). Peak 989 TFLOPS dense BF16; 35% model-FLOP utilisation for training and scoring, 15% for generation. That makes one training token $1.4e-08 and one generated token $1.1e-08 per 1B parameters, so 1T training tokens per 1B parameters is $14k. Change the price or utilisation and every number rescales together.

价格。H100 SXM 按 $3.00/GPU 小时，即 2026 年 9 月按需市场中位数（聚合站约 $3.4；市场型供应商 $2–3；超大云 $7–12）。峰值 989 TFLOPS 稠密 BF16；训练与打分按 35% 的模型 FLOP 利用率，生成按 15%。于是每 1B 参数每个训练 token 约 $1.4e-08，每个生成 token 约 $1.1e-08，每 1B 参数训 1T token 约 $14k。改价格或利用率，所有数字一起缩放。

What it hides. Environment latency and idle GPUs in agentic RL, CPU-side work, API markups over raw compute, and the wall-clock of long-context attention beyond the multipliers noted. Read the numbers as order-of-magnitude, and read the basis column before quoting one.

它看不见的。agentic RL 里的环境延迟和 GPU 空转、CPU 侧的工作、API 相对原始算力的溢价、以及长上下文注意力超出所注倍数的墙钟时间。把这些数当作数量级来读，引用之前先看依据一列。

People. Three labour lines, summed, not averaged: build (research-scientist hours to design and implement the method on a standard stack, $80/h), tune (ML-engineer hours to launch, watch, debug and re-run, $65/h), and annotation (items × minutes per item, at $31/h for trained general annotators or $70/h for STEM, clinical or PhD-level experts). Wages are US means for September 2026, chosen over medians on purpose: the stack is new and the work is hard. Human cost is not divided by model size, because people do not scale with parameters; for a frontier pretraining run the tune line is a team's months. Where a recipe reuses a public dataset the annotation is still counted at what it would cost to produce, and the basis line says so.

人。三项相加而不是平均：搭建（研究科学家在标准栈上设计与实现方法的小时数，$80/h）、调通（工程师启动、盯曲线、调试、重跑的小时数，$65/h）、标注（条数 × 每条分钟数，受训通用标注员 $31/h，理工、临床或博士级专家 $70/h）。时薪取 2026 年 9 月的美国平均值，特意不取中位数：技术栈新、活难。人力不按模型尺寸归一，人不随参数量缩放；对前沿预训练来说调通一项就是一个团队的几个月。配方复用公开数据集的，标注仍按自己产出的成本计，依据一列会注明。

Take a training pipeline apart into the steps a person once had to do by hand. For post-training that is InstructGPT's six steps plus the five an agent needs: collect prompts, write demonstrations, sample candidates, compare, model the reward, optimise; design the environment, demonstrate trajectories, judge each step, verify the outcome, run the curriculum. For pretraining it is GPT-3's nine: acquire the corpus, clean it, tokenise, mix, the objective, allocate compute, schedule, and since 2024 anneal and extend context, then evaluate.

把一条训练管线拆成人曾经必须手工做的步骤。后训练是 InstructGPT 的六步加上 agent 需要的五步：收集 prompt、写示范、采样候选、两两比较、建奖励模型、优化策略；搭环境、写轨迹示范、逐步打分、验证结果、安排课程。预训练是 GPT-3 的九步：取语料、清洗、分词、配比、目标函数、算力分配、schedule，2024 年起多出退火与扩上下文，最后评估选点。

Every method on the two maps below replaces one or more of those cells: a model writes the demonstrations, a program checks the answer, a rubric stands in for the annotator, the model's own samples stand in for the critic. Read down a column and you see how one step was automated over four years; read across a row and you see what a given year still asked of people.

下面两张图上的每个方法，都在替换其中一格或几格：模型代替人写示范，程序代替人对答案，rubric 代替标注员，模型自己的采样代替 critic。顺着一列往下看，是一个步骤在四年里如何被自动化；顺着一行横着看，是某一年还向人要什么。

So the question in the title has a concrete answer. By 2026 the human cells left are not labour but judgement: the seed set, the rubric audit, the environment and tool interface, whether the verifier can be trusted, and the final say on what to train and when to stop. The distance left is the distance from doing to deciding. Click any cell for the same method written two ways, in symbols and for grandma, plus what usually breaks first.

于是题目里的问题有一个具体的答案。到 2026 年，留给人的格子已经不是劳动而是判断：种子集、rubric 审核、环境与工具接口、验证器可不可信、以及训什么与何时停的终审。剩下的距离，是从“做”到“决定”的距离。点开任意一格，可以看同一个方法的两种写法，符号版和奶奶版，以及它通常先坏在哪。

My take我的看法

RLHF set out with a narrow ambition: make a dialogue model say what people prefer. Its reward was a proxy learned from comparisons, trustworthy only near the policy that produced them, and the KL leash existed to keep the model there. Agents changed the object being trained from an answer to a trajectory, and brought executable, verifiable environments with them. Once the world can be run and the outcome checked, the reward no longer has to be a learned guess, and the maps below show the field spending three years cashing that in: verifiers instead of reward models, rubrics where verifiers do not reach, curricula that move with the policy.

RLHF 最初的抱负很窄：让一个对话模型说出人更喜欢的话。它的奖励是从比较里学出来的代理，只在产生这些比较的策略附近可信，KL 这根绳子就是为了把模型拴在那里。Agent 把被训练的对象从一个回答换成一条轨迹，并带来了可执行、可验证的环境。一旦世界可以跑起来、结果可以核对，奖励就不必再是学出来的猜测，下面两张图里这个领域花了三年把这件事兑现：验证器代替奖励模型，验证器够不到的地方用 rubric，课程随策略移动。

I think this is where it ends up: not a sequence of one-off runs, each with its own dataset and its own reward, but one long-lived objective of continuously building the model's understanding of the world it acts in and of what counts as reward there. The environment and the verifier become the things that keep being built; the model persists across tasks instead of being trained for each one. Human leverage retreats to the few cells that decide what the world is and what counts as good in it, and stays there.

我认为最终会走到这里：不再是一次次各带数据集、各带奖励的一次性训练，而是一个长久存在的目标，持续构建模型对它所处世界的理解、以及对什么算作奖励的理解。环境和验证器成为持续被构建的东西；模型跨任务持续存在，而不是为每个任务各训一次。人的杠杆退到少数几格，决定世界是什么、其中什么算好，并留在那里。

How to read the maps读图要点

  • Pretraining's evolution barely touches P5: the loss is next-token MLE from 2018 to 2026; MTP and FIM add an auxiliary term or reorder the sequence. The real change is in P2/P4 (data), P6 (compute allocation), P7 (schedule) and the newly grown P8.预训练的演进几乎不碰 P5：损失形式从 2018 到 2026 都是 next-token MLE，MTP 与 FIM 只是在它旁边加辅助项或改序列顺序。真正的变化在 P2/P4（数据）、P6（算力分配）、P7（schedule）以及新长出来的 P8。
  • P8 exists because P7 changed: under cosine-to-the-end there is no natural point to swap data late in the run; WSD makes annealing a pluggable stage, and only then can mid-training be named.P8 的出现依赖 P7 的变化：cosine-to-end 下无法在末期换数据，WSD 让“退火”成为可插拔阶段，mid-training 才能被命名。
