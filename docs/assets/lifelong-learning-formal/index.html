Lifelong Learning, Formally: Objects, Boundaries, Schools终身学习的形式化：对象、边界、流派

2026-05-01lifelong-learningformal-modelnon-stationaryregret

What has to be written down要写清楚的是什么

The world an agent lives in changes its rules from time to time. The agent is far smaller than that world. And some of what it knows it cannot afford to lose. Lifelong learning is the problem of doing well across the whole sequence of rule changes under those three facts. Each fact has a home in an existing theory: how fast rules may drift is a variation budget from non-stationary optimisation; an agent smaller than its world is the premise of bounded rationality and of the big-world hypothesis; knowledge that must survive every change is what constrained learning calls a constraint. What did not exist was one statement that holds all three at once and says what "doing well" means against a fair ruler.

智能体所在的世界会不时更换规则；智能体远比世界小；它知道的东西里有一部分丢不起。终身学习就是在这三个事实下，跨越整串规则更替仍然做得好。三个事实各有出处：规则能漂移多快，是非平稳优化里的变动预算；智能体小于世界，是有界理性和"大世界假说"的前提；必须跨越每次变化活下来的知识，在约束学习里叫约束。缺的是把三者同时放进一句话，并且说清楚拿什么尺子量"做得好"。

Each neighbouring paradigm holds one piece and drops the others. Continual learning asks "do not forget" but never states how fast the world is allowed to change. Non-stationary online learning states exactly that, through the variation budget, but has no notion of knowledge that must not be lost. General continual reinforcement learning admits any non-stationarity at all, including worlds that change too fast to learn. Computational mechanics says precisely what should be remembered, the causal states, but has no reward and no behaviour. And the usual ruler, an unconstrained per-period optimum, measures mostly the size gap between the agent and an ideal, not the learning gap.

相邻范式各拿一块、丢掉其余。持续学习问"别忘"，却从不说世界允许变多快；非平稳在线学习用变动预算把这一点说得很准，却没有"不能丢的知识"这个对象；一般化的持续强化学习什么非平稳都收，包括快到学不了的世界；计算力学精确地说了该记什么，也就是因果态，却没有奖励也没有行为。而常用的尺子，无约束的逐期最优，量到的主要是智能体和理想之间的容量差，不是学习差。

So the framework below invents no new mathematics. It makes three moves with borrowed parts. It defines core information backwards, as whatever costs you unbounded regret if it goes, so that the object connects to reward and behaviour rather than to prediction alone. It replaces the ruler with an oracle of the agent's own size that is simply told the current rules, so that regret measures learning and retention, not capacity. And it cuts the problem out of its neighbours with three criteria that must all hold: order matters, the world drifts sub-linearly but not zero, and the core set is non-empty. Everything else, the twelve schools of method, is existing work sorted by which of three challenges it answers.

所以下面的框架不发明新数学，只用借来的零件做三个动作。把核心信息倒过来定义：丢了会让 regret 无限增长的，就是核心，这样这个对象接的是奖励和行为，不只是预测。把尺子换成一个和智能体同等大小、只是被告知当前规则的预言机，这样 regret 量的是学习和保留，不是容量。再用三条必须同时成立的判据把问题从相邻范式里切出来：顺序不可换、世界亚线性但非零地漂移、核心集非空。剩下的十二个方法流派都是已有工作，按它们回答三个挑战中的哪一个来归类。

My take: the real score is survival我的看法：真正的分数是活多久

What lifelong learning should really measure is survival in an uncertain environment. The severity of environmental change has to be made explicit. The score can be a reward, or it can be an accumulation. And the agent has to be forced to understand that it should explore widely while it has a surplus, and adapt hard and stay alive when the environment shifts violently. The one that lives longest wins.

终身学习真正要衡量的，是不确定环境下的存活。要明确环境变化的剧烈程度；可以记奖励，也可以记累积；要迫使智能体懂得在有余粮的时候多探索，在环境剧变的时候全力适应、活下来。活得长才是胜利者。

The framework below still compares regret at a given horizon T. In the survival view T is not given; it is the outcome of behaviour. Returns multiply rather than add, and the split between exploring and adapting is set by the statistics of the environment's switches. That step is motivation here, not definition. The nearest prior work is population growth rate in fluctuating environments, and agents for which mortality is part of the model.

下面的框架仍是在给定视界 T 下比 regret。存活视角里 T 不是给定的，是行为的结果；回报从相加变成相乘，探索与适应的比例由环境切换的统计决定。这一步在本文里只是动机，不是定义。最近的前人是波动环境中的种群增长率，和把死亡写进模型的智能体。

D1.3

Regime-generating family体制生成分布族

\[\Phi^{(k)} \sim \mu_{t_k},\qquad \mu_\cdot : \mathbb R_+ \to \Delta(\mathcal P),\qquad \mu_\cdot \in \mathcal M\]

Regimes are drawn at random: each new regime is a sample from a distribution over the regime space, and that distribution itself is a trajectory in time belonging to an admissible family.

体制是随机抽出来的：每个新体制从体制空间上的一个分布里抽样，而这个分布本身随时间走出一条轨迹，属于某个容许族。

In plain words直白地说 There is a hidden lottery machine that decides what the next rulebook is. The machine itself may slowly change over time. "The machine as it is at time t" is the generating distribution.有一台隐藏的抽奖机决定下一套规则是什么。抽奖机本身也可能随时间慢慢变。"时刻 t 那台抽奖机的样子"，就是生成分布。

Lineage来源 A distribution over environments as the object of intelligence (Legg & Hutter 2007); a time-varying one is the setting of non-stationary stochastic optimisation (Besbes, Gur & Zeevi 2015).把"环境上的分布"当作智能的对象（Legg & Hutter 2007）；让它随时间变，是非平稳随机优化的设定（Besbes, Gur & Zeevi 2015）。

D1.4

Variation budget变动预算

\[V_T(\mu_\cdot) := \sum_{k=1}^{T-1} d_{TV}\big(\mu_{t_k}, \mu_{t_{k+1}}\big)\]
\[\mathcal M_\rho := \{\mu_\cdot : V_T(\mu_\cdot) \le \rho(T)\ \text{ for all } T\}\]

How much the generating distribution may move over a horizon, summed in total-variation distance. The admissible family is parameterised by a budget function of the horizon.

生成分布在一个视界内总共允许移动多少，用总变差距离累加。容许族由一个关于视界的预算函数参数化。

In plain words直白地说 We measure how much the lottery machine is allowed to change over time. If it may change arbitrarily fast, nothing can be learned; if it changes slowly, there is a chance to adapt. The budget is that allowance.我们度量抽奖机随时间被允许改变多少。它可以任意快地变，就什么都学不了；变得慢，就有机会适应。变动预算就是这个允许量。

Lineage来源 Directly from Besbes, Gur & Zeevi 2015, where the budget sets the achievable regret rate. The static ancestor is Ashby's law of requisite variety (1956): a regulator needs at least as much variety as its disturbances.直接来自 Besbes, Gur & Zeevi 2015，那里预算决定可达的 regret 率。更早的静态版本是 Ashby 的必要多样性定律（1956）：调节器的多样性不能低于扰动。

D1.5

Environment levels环境层级

\[\text{Level 0: } \rho(T) = \Omega(T)\qquad \text{Level 1: } 0 < \rho(T) = o(T)\qquad \text{Level 2: } \rho(T) = 0\]

Three levels of the regime process: arbitrary non-stationarity with no learnable structure; non-stationary but sub-linear change; stationary. Lifelong learning is defined on Levels 1 and 2. Level 0 is excluded because no agent can beat chance in expectation there.

体制过程分三级：任意非平稳、没有可学结构；非平稳但变化亚线性；平稳。终身学习定义在第 1、2 级上。第 0 级排除，因为在期望意义下没有智能体能胜过随机。

In plain words直白地说 Three worlds. In the first the rules change so wildly that nobody can learn. In the second the rules change, but with restraint. In the third the overall climate is fixed and only the weather varies. Lifelong learning lives in the last two.三个世界。第一个里规则变得太疯狂，无人能学。第二个里规则会变但有节制。第三个里整体"气候"不变，只有具体事件在变。终身学习活在后两个世界里。

Lineage来源 The learnability threshold is the sub-linear-budget condition of Besbes, Gur & Zeevi 2015; general continual RL (Khetarpal et al. 2022) does not impose it, which is exactly where this framework narrows it.可学习门槛就是 Besbes, Gur & Zeevi 2015 的亚线性预算条件；一般化的持续强化学习（Khetarpal et al. 2022）不加这个条件，这正是本框架收窄的地方。

C2

Criterion C2: structured non-stationarity判据 C2：结构化非平稳

\[V_T(\mu_\cdot) > 0 \qquad \text{and} \qquad V_T(\mu_\cdot) = o(T)\]

The generating distribution moves, but its total movement grows sub-linearly in the horizon. This places the problem at Level 1, or on its boundary with Level 2.

生成分布在动，但总动量随视界亚线性增长。这把问题放在第 1 级，或第 1 级与第 2 级的边界上。

In plain words直白地说 The world keeps changing, but not madly. There is always enough consistency to learn from, and never enough to learn once and stop.世界一直在变，但不是疯狂地变。总有足够的一致性可供学习，但也从不足以学一次就停。

Lineage来源 Besbes, Gur & Zeevi 2015. Bayesian RL and online meta-learning usually assume zero budget; standard continual-learning benchmarks often impose near-linear budgets, which puts them outside.Besbes, Gur & Zeevi 2015。贝叶斯强化学习和在线元学习通常假设预算为零；常见持续学习基准常施加近线性预算，因此落在外面。

H1

Difficulty dimension: drift rate难度维度：漂移速度

The criteria classify a problem; they do not say how hard it is. The first of three dimensions that do is the growth rate of the variation budget: how fast the world changes.

判据只做分类，不说难度。决定难度的三个维度中，第一个是变动预算的增长率：世界变多快。

S3.5a

Benchmark need: a knob on the budget基准需求：变动预算旋钮

The variation budget has to be a parameter of the benchmark, so that Level 1 can be swept from near-stationary to near-Level 0 and regret scaling measured against it.

变动预算必须是基准的参数，这样才能把第 1 级从近平稳扫到接近第 0 级，并对照它测 regret 的增长。

D1.1

Regime体制

\[\Phi = (\mathcal S, \mathcal A, T, R, O) \in \mathcal P\]

A regime fixes, for one stretch of time, the state space, the action space, the transition dynamics, the reward function and the observation function of the environment. Two regimes differ as soon as any one component differs.

一个体制在一段固定时间内规定环境的状态空间、动作空间、转移动态、奖励函数和观测函数。任一组件不同，就是不同的体制。

In plain words直白地说 A regime is the complete rulebook of the game you are in right now: what you can see, what you can do, what counts as winning and losing. When the rulebook changes, the regime has changed.体制就是当下这局游戏的完整规则：你能看到什么、能做什么、怎样算赢、怎样算输。规则一变，体制就变了。

Lineage来源 The hidden observation function is the POMDP view (Kaelbling, Littman & Cassandra 1998); a sequence of MDPs as the unit of change follows continual RL (Khetarpal, Riemer, Rish & Precup 2022).观测函数对智能体隐藏，是 POMDP 的视角（Kaelbling, Littman & Cassandra 1998）；以一串 MDP 为变化单位，沿用持续强化学习（Khetarpal, Riemer, Rish & Precup 2022）。

D1.2

Regime sequence体制序列

\[\{\Phi^{(k)}\}_{k=1}^{\infty},\qquad 0 = t_1 < t_2 < \cdots,\qquad \Phi^{(k)} \text{ in force on } [t_k, t_{k+1})\]

A countable sequence of regimes with time stamps; the k-th regime is in force on the k-th interval. The agent never sees k or the stamps directly.

带时间戳的可数体制序列；第 k 个体制在第 k 个区间内生效。智能体既看不到 k，也看不到时间戳。

In plain words直白地说 The world does not stay on one rulebook forever. New rules arrive at moments nobody announces. The whole chain of rulebooks is the regime sequence.世界不会永远停在同一套规则上。新规则在没人通知的时刻出现。整串规则连起来，就是体制序列。

Lineage来源 Switching / piecewise-stationary environments in online learning (Herbster & Warmuth 1998; Garivier & Moulines 2011).在线学习里的切换环境、分段平稳环境（Herbster & Warmuth 1998；Garivier & Moulines 2011）。

H3

Difficulty dimension: regime identifiability难度维度：体制可识别性

The third dimension: how many observations it takes to infer which regime is in force from its effects. The trade-offs among the three dimensions are open; the schools below take different positions on them.

第三个维度：从体制的效应推断它是哪一个，需要多少观测。三个维度之间的权衡是开放问题；下面的流派在这些权衡上立场不同。

A-1

School A-1: change-point detection流派 A-1：变化点检测

What it is是什么 Classical statistics: CUSUM (Page 1954), Bayesian online change-point detection (Adams & MacKay 2007), sliding-window UCB for switching bandits (Garivier & Moulines 2011), window-based non-stationary optimisation (Besbes, Gur & Zeevi 2015). Strong guarantees, cheap, anytime variants exist.经典统计：CUSUM（Page 1954）、贝叶斯在线变化点检测（Adams & MacKay 2007）、切换 bandit 的滑窗 UCB（Garivier & Moulines 2011）、基于窗口的非平稳优化（Besbes, Gur & Zeevi 2015）。保证强、开销小、有 anytime 变体。

Limit here在终身学习里的局限 It detects change in the observation distribution, but a regime may change in components that do not show up in observations at once (reward, transitions). Extending it to a hidden regime representation is not direct.它检测的是观测分布的变化，但体制可能在不立即反映于观测的组件上变（奖励、转移）。推广到隐体制表示并不直接。

Way in改造路径 Run the detector in the agent's belief space rather than the observation space: what has to shift is the belief over the regime.在智能体的信念空间而不是观测空间做检测：需要转变的是对体制的信念分布。

A-2

School A-2: latent-regime modelling流派 A-2：隐体制建模

What it is是什么 Treat the regime as a latent variable: hidden Markov models, switching state-space models, Bayesian policies with latent variables. Principled uncertainty, natural online inference by variational methods or particle filters.把体制当作潜变量：隐马尔可夫模型、switching state-space 模型、带隐变量的贝叶斯策略。不确定性量化有原理，在线推断（变分、粒子滤波）自然。

Limit here在终身学习里的局限 Hidden Markov models assume Markov transitions between regimes; the generating trajectory may depend on history in richer ways. Non-parametric extensions handle open-ended regime sets at a high computational price.隐马尔可夫模型假设体制之间马尔可夫转移；生成轨迹可能有更复杂的历史依赖。非参扩展能处理开放式体制集，但计算代价很大。

Way in改造路径 History-dependent switching models with non-parametric priors: trade compute for expressiveness.带非参先验的历史依赖切换模型：用算力换表达力。

A-3

School A-3: context-based meta-inference流派 A-3：基于上下文的元推断

What it is是什么 Keep a context vector summarising recent experience and let a meta-learned function map it to a regime representation; in-context learning in transformers is an instance. No explicit generative model needed; works when meta-training can simulate many regimes.维护一个汇总近期经验的上下文向量，由元学习到的函数把它映到体制表示；transformer 的 in-context learning 就是一个实例。不需要显式生成模型；元训练能模拟大量体制时有效。

Limit here在终身学习里的局限 The meta-training regime distribution must cover deployment; a shift in the generating trajectory between the two is a silent failure mode.元训练的体制分布必须覆盖部署分布；两者之间生成轨迹的偏移是静默失败模式。

Way in改造路径 Continual meta-training: treat the meta-learning process itself as a lifelong-learning problem one level up.持续元训练：把元学习过程本身当作更高一层的终身学习问题。

A-4

School A-4: intrinsic novelty detection流派 A-4：内在新颖性检测

What it is是什么 The agent watches its own prediction error; a spike signals a regime switch. Related to curiosity-driven exploration and surprise-based learning. No separate detector: the agent is its own signal.智能体监控自己的预测误差，误差飙升即体制切换的信号。与好奇心驱动探索、基于惊奇的学习相关。不需要单独的检测器，智能体自身就是信号源。

Limit here在终身学习里的局限 It confuses a real regime change with internal model error. A badly trained agent is always surprised; a well-trained one is surprised only by a new regime. Telling the two apart is hard.它混淆了真实的体制变化与内部模型误差。训练不好的智能体总是"惊讶"；训练好的只在新体制前惊讶。两者难分。

Way in改造路径 Add an expected-error baseline under the known regime, and separate error from ignorance (high belief entropy) from error from being contradicted (low entropy, wrong prediction). This is also the oldest form of the idea: when the current model class fails to compress, innovate a richer one (Crutchfield 1994).配一个已知体制下的期望误差基线，区分"无知导致的误差"（信念熵高）与"被冒犯导致的误差"（熵低但预测错）。这也是这个想法最早的形态：当前模型类压不住观测时，跃迁到更丰富的模型类（Crutchfield 1994）。

D1.8

Bounded-capacity oracle有界容量预言机

\[\pi^*_{BC}(\Phi) := \arg\max_{\pi \in \Pi_{N,C}:\ \mathcal I_{core} \subseteq \mathcal I(\pi)}\ \mathbb E_{\xi \sim P_\Phi}\big[R(\pi, \Phi, \xi)\big]\]
\[R^*_{BC}(\Phi) := \mathbb E_{\xi \sim P_\Phi}\big[R(\pi^*_{BC}(\Phi), \Phi, \xi)\big]\]

For each regime, the best policy that fits in the same parameter and compute budget as the agent and still holds the core set. It is handed the regime for free, reconfigures at zero cost, never sees the future and never learns. It is a reference, not a player.

对每个体制，在与智能体相同的参数量和算力预算内、且仍然持有核心集的最优策略。它免费拿到体制，零代价重配参数，看不到未来，也从不学习。它是参照，不是选手。

In plain words直白地说 Imagine an ideal student with exactly the same brain and compute as ours, plus one magic power: the moment the rules are revealed, it instantly rewires itself into the best possible player, without ever losing its core identity. It does not exist, but it tells us what our resources could do in principle.想象一个理想学生，脑子和算力都和我们的学生一样大，只多一项魔法：规则一揭晓，它就瞬间把自己重塑成最佳玩家，同时从不丢掉核心身份。它并不存在，但它告诉我们：用这点资源，理论上能做到什么程度。

Lineage来源 Bounded optimality (Russell 1997; Horvitz 2001) supplies the "same size" idea; the per-period optimum of dynamic regret (Besbes, Gur & Zeevi 2015) and the best switching expert (Herbster & Warmuth 1998) are the unconstrained rulers this one tightens."同等大小"来自有界最优性（Russell 1997；Horvitz 2001）；动态 regret 的逐期最优（Besbes, Gur & Zeevi 2015）和最优切换专家（Herbster & Warmuth 1998）是这把尺子收紧之前的无约束版本。

R1

Why this ruler为什么用这把尺子

The usual comparator in dynamic regret is the unconstrained per-period optimum. Against it, a small agent loses mostly by being small, and the regret says little about learning. Against an oracle of the agent's own size that must also hold the core set, the remaining gap is exactly what a learner could have closed: identifying the regime sooner, adapting within it faster, and not dropping what it must keep.

动态 regret 常用的比较对象是无约束的逐期最优。对着它，小智能体输主要是因为小，regret 说明不了学习。对着一个和智能体同等大小、还必须持有核心集的预言机，剩下的差距恰恰是学习者本可以缩小的：更早认出体制、在体制内更快适应、不丢必须保住的东西。

S3.5c

Benchmark need: a computable oracle基准需求：可计算的预言机

A reference model of the same size as the agent, given the regime as an oracle, so that the bounded-capacity benchmark is a number rather than a definition.

一个和智能体同规模、被告知体制的参考模型，让有界容量基准成为一个数，而不只是一个定义。

D1.9

Agent policy智能体策略

\[\pi : \mathcal I_{agent}(t) \times \mathrm{History}_t \to \Delta(\mathcal A),\qquad \pi \in \Pi_{N,C}\]

The agent is a policy inside the resource-constrained class, mapping its current information state and interaction history to a distribution over actions. Every action serves sensing, prediction, learning and execution at once; no separation of function is imposed.

智能体是资源约束类里的一个策略，把当前信息状态和交互历史映到动作分布。每个动作同时承担感知、预测、学习、执行；不强加功能分离。

In plain words直白地说 A real agent has a finite brain and finite compute. Every move it makes is at the same time looking, guessing, learning and acting. These are not four separate jobs; they are one decision seen from four sides.真实的智能体只有有限的脑子和算力。它做的每个动作，同时在观察、预测、学习、行动。这不是四件事，是同一个决策从四个角度看。

Lineage来源 The agent-is-smaller-than-the-world premise is the big-world hypothesis (Javed & Sutton 2024); information-processing cost as the price of a decision is Ortega & Braun 2013 and Genewein et al. 2015."智能体远小于世界"是大世界假说（Javed & Sutton 2024）；把信息处理成本写成决策的代价，是 Ortega & Braun 2013 与 Genewein et al. 2015。

D1.10

Reward structure奖励结构

\[R_k(\pi, \xi) = r_k^{det}(\pi, \Phi^{(k)}) + u(\pi, \Phi^{(k)}, \xi),\qquad R_k(\pi) := \mathbb E_{\xi \sim P_{\Phi^{(k)}}}\big[R_k(\pi, \xi)\big]\]

Reward in a regime splits into a part fixed by the policy and the regime, and a part driven by exogenous randomness the agent does not control. Expected reward averages over the latter.

一个体制下的奖励分成两部分：由策略和体制决定的部分，以及由智能体控制不了的外生随机性驱动的部分。期望奖励对后者取平均。

In plain words直白地说 Some rewards you get just by doing the right thing. Others depend on things you cannot control, the weather, other people. We count both, and the expected reward is our honest estimate of the average outcome.有些奖励做对事就能拿到。另一些取决于你控制不了的事，天气、别人的行为。两种都算，期望奖励是对平均结果的诚实估计。

Lineage来源 Standard in stochastic control; the individual-level randomness that population models treat separately (Rivoire & Leibler 2011) is the same split.随机控制里的标准写法；种群模型单独处理的个体层随机性（Rivoire & Leibler 2011）是同一种拆分。

C1

Criterion C1: order matters判据 C1：时间序列性

\[\mathbb E\big[R_k(\pi;\ \mathcal I_{agent}(t_k))\big] \text{ depends on } \mathcal I_{agent}(t_k), \text{ itself fixed by the history up to } t_k\]

There is a time index along which samples or tasks cannot be freely reordered: what the agent knows at a given time is a function of everything before it, and reward depends on that.

存在一个时间索引，样本或任务的处理顺序沿着它不可自由交换：智能体在某一时刻知道什么，是此前一切的函数，而奖励依赖于此。

In plain words直白地说 Time is real. You cannot shuffle the order in which things happen: the student at step ten was shaped by the first nine steps.时间有意义。你不能打乱事情发生的顺序：第十步的学生是由前九步塑造出来的。

Lineage来源 Shared by all online learning; batch meta-learning violates it by allowing task order to be permuted.所有在线学习都满足；批量元学习允许任务顺序交换，因此违反它。

C-1

School C-1: meta-learned initialisation流派 C-1：元学习初始化

What it is是什么 Learn initial parameters that adapt fast: MAML (Finn, Abbeel & Levine 2017), Reptile (Nichol, Achiam & Schulman 2018), Meta-SGD (Li et al. 2017), ANIL (Raghu et al. 2020). Clean theory; works in well-studied domains.学一个能快速适应的初始参数：MAML（Finn, Abbeel & Levine 2017）、Reptile（Nichol, Achiam & Schulman 2018）、Meta-SGD（Li et al. 2017）、ANIL（Raghu et al. 2020）。理论干净，在研究充分的领域有效。

Limit here在终身学习里的局限 Assumes a stationary task distribution; under a moving generator the learned initialisation itself goes stale.假设任务分布平稳；抽奖机在动时，学到的初始化本身会过时。

Way in改造路径 Make the initialisation a function of the estimated generating distribution and run the outer loop online as it moves: nested online learning, outer loop tracking the generator, inner loop adapting to the current regime.让初始化成为估计出的生成分布的函数，外循环随它在线运行：嵌套在线学习，外循环追踪抽奖机，内循环适应当前体制。

C-2

School C-2: learned optimisers流派 C-2：学习优化规则

What it is是什么 Meta-learn the optimisation procedure itself: RL² (Duan et al. 2016), learning to learn by gradient descent by gradient descent (Andrychowicz et al. 2016). More expressive than a learned initialisation: the update rule itself adapts.元学习优化过程本身：RL²（Duan et al. 2016）、learning to learn by gradient descent by gradient descent（Andrychowicz et al. 2016）。比元学习初始化更具表达力，更新规则本身会适应。

Limit here在终身学习里的局限 Double meta-learning compounds instability; a learned rule can fail silently under a new generator.双重元学习会放大不稳定；学到的规则在新的生成分布下可能静默失败。

Way in改造路径 Hierarchical meta-learning: an outer process tracks the generator and modulates the learned rule.分层元学习：由外层过程追踪生成分布并调节学到的学习规则。

C-3

School C-3: in-context adaptation流派 C-3：In-context 适应

What it is是什么 No parameter update during adaptation: infer the regime from the prompt and adapt in activation space; transformer few-shot learning, prompting, in-context learning. Gradient-free adaptation is cheap and reversible, suited to fast switches.适应过程中不更新参数：从 prompt 推断体制，在激活空间中完成适应；transformer 的 few-shot、prompting、ICL。零梯度适应便宜且可逆，适合快速切换。

Limit here在终身学习里的局限 Context is finite; long-horizon core information cannot live in it indefinitely. Needs the memory school (B-3).上下文长度有限；长视界的核心信息不能无限驻留其中。需要和记忆流派（B-3）整合。

Way in改造路径 Hybrid: the core set lives in memory and is retrieved every step, regime-specific information lives in context, adaptation happens in activations. Architecturally the most complete path.混合模式：核心集驻留记忆、每步检索；体制专用信息驻留上下文；适应在激活空间完成。这是架构上最完整的路径。

C-4

School C-4: continual fine-tuning流派 C-4：持续微调

What it is是什么 Plain gradient descent on the stream with some forgetting: incremental low-rank adapters, adapter tuning with selective freezing. Simple; reuses all standard infrastructure.在数据流上做标准梯度下降，带一定遗忘：增量低秩适配、带选择性冻结的 adapter 调优。简单，复用全部标准基础设施。

Limit here在终身学习里的局限 Catastrophic forgetting and loss of plasticity are documented failure modes; it must be paired with a retention mechanism from challenge B.灾难性遗忘和可塑性丧失是记录在案的失效模式；必须与挑战 B 的机制配合。

Way in改造路径 A bank of adapters routed by the estimated regime, one per detected regime, gated by the challenge-A identifier.按估计体制路由的 adapter 组：每个检测到的体制一组，由挑战 A 的识别器门控。

D1.6

Information content of a policy策略的信息内容

\[\mathcal I(\pi) := \{\, I : \mathrm{CausalInfluence}(I \to B_t) \ge \eta \,\}\]

A piece of information is "in" a policy when removing it, with everything else held fixed, changes the policy's behaviour distribution by at least a threshold. Presence is read off by counterfactual ablation, not by inspection.

一条信息"在"策略里，当且仅当把它拿掉、其他一切不动，策略的行为分布变化不小于某个阈值。在不在，由反事实消融读出，不靠肉眼检查。

In plain words直白地说 A piece of knowledge counts as really being inside a policy only if taking it away actually changes what the policy does. Knowledge that sits there and changes nothing does not count.一条知识只有在拿掉它会实际改变策略行为时，才算真正在策略里。摆在那里却对行为毫无影响的，不算。

Lineage来源 Presence-by-intervention is the causal-inference notion; the ablation-and-editing experiments of the knowledge-localisation line (Meng et al. 2022) are its empirical form."在不在由干预决定"是因果推断的用法；知识定位那条线的编辑与消融实验（Meng et al. 2022）是它的实验形态。

D1.7

Core information核心信息

\[\mathcal I_{core} := \Big\{\, I \in \mathcal I^* : \exists\, \Phi \in \mathcal P,\ \lim_{T \to \infty} \mathbb E\big[\mathrm{Regret}_T \mid I \notin \mathcal I(\pi)\big] = \infty \Big\}\]

Core information is the set of pieces whose loss makes regret grow without bound in some regime. The definition is forward-looking: it is about future regret, not about past tasks.

核心信息是这样一些信息：在某个体制下丢掉它，regret 就会无限增长。这个定义是前瞻的：看的是未来的 regret，不是过去的任务。

In plain words直白地说 Some knowledge is like a mother tongue: lose it and whole stretches of life stop working. Other knowledge is replaceable: lose it and learn it again. Core information is the first kind, the kind you cannot afford to let go.有些知识像母语：丢了它，生活整块整块地走不通。另一些是可替换的：丢了再学就是。核心信息是前一种，你承受不起让它消失。

Lineage来源 The cleanest earlier notion is the causal state of computational mechanics, the minimal sufficient statistic of the past for the future (Crutchfield & Young 1989; Shalizi & Crutchfield 2001), defined by prediction rather than by regret. Knoblauch, Husain & Diethe 2020 show optimal continual learning needs perfect memory of what matters; the two definitions coincide when reward depends on the past only through that statistic.更早、更干净的概念是计算力学的因果态：对预测未来而言，关于过去的最小充分统计量（Crutchfield & Young 1989；Shalizi & Crutchfield 2001），按预测而非 regret 定义。Knoblauch, Husain & Diethe 2020 证明最优持续学习需要对要紧的东西有完美记忆；当奖励只通过该统计量依赖过去时，两种定义重合。

C3

Criterion C3: a non-empty core判据 C3：核心信息非空

\[\mathcal I_{core} \neq \emptyset\]

There exists knowledge whose loss makes regret diverge. Without such knowledge there is nothing that has to be carried across changes, and the problem is just non-stationary RL.

存在丢失后会使 regret 发散的知识。没有这种知识，就没有必须跨越变化带下去的东西，问题就只是非平稳强化学习。

In plain words直白地说 Something has to be carried through every change, or the student eventually becomes useless. If your problem has no such thing, you do not have a lifelong-learning problem.有些东西必须贯穿每一次变化被带下去，否则学生最终会变得没用。如果你的问题里不存在这种东西，那你就没有终身学习问题。

Lineage来源 Constrained learning (Altman 1999) and the perfect-memory result of Knoblauch, Husain & Diethe 2020; non-stationary RL has no such object.约束学习（Altman 1999）和 Knoblauch, Husain & Diethe 2020 的完美记忆结果；非平稳强化学习没有这个对象。

H2

Difficulty dimension: core share of capacity难度维度：核心占容量的比例

\[|\mathcal I_{core}| / N\]

The second dimension: how much of the agent's capacity is locked up by retention and how much is left for adapting. Loss of plasticity under continued training (Dohare et al. 2024) is the empirical face of this trade-off.

第二个维度：智能体的容量有多少被保留占住、多少留给适应。持续训练下的可塑性丢失（Dohare et al. 2024）是这个权衡的实证面。

B-1

School B-1: regularisation-based retention流派 B-1：基于正则化的保留

What it is是什么 Identify parameters important to past tasks and penalise changing them: elastic weight consolidation (Kirkpatrick et al. 2017), synaptic intelligence (Zenke, Poole & Ganguli 2017), memory-aware synapses (Aljundi et al. 2018). Simple; drops into standard training.识别对过去任务重要的参数并惩罚其变化：EWC（Kirkpatrick et al. 2017）、Synaptic Intelligence（Zenke, Poole & Ganguli 2017）、Memory Aware Synapses（Aljundi et al. 2018）。简单，可直接加进标准训练。

Limit here在终身学习里的局限 "Importance" here is retrospective, defined by past tasks. Core information is prospective, defined by future regret. A parameter can be unimportant for every past regime and decisive for a future one.这里的"重要性"是回溯的，由过去任务定义。核心信息是前瞻的，由未来 regret 定义。某个参数可能对所有过去体制都不重要，却对未来某个体制至关重要。

Way in改造路径 Replace Fisher information by counterfactual importance: for each parameter, estimate the regret increment under hypothetical regime perturbations.把 Fisher 信息换成反事实重要性：对每个参数，在假设性的体制扰动下估计 regret 增量。

B-2

School B-2: modular architectures流派 B-2：模块化架构

What it is是什么 Separate core and adaptive modules structurally: progressive networks (Rusu et al. 2016), mixture-of-experts routing (Shazeer et al. 2017), PackNet (Mallya & Lazebnik 2018), frozen backbones with adapters. Retention is enforced by the architecture: a frozen module cannot be overwritten.在结构上分离核心模块与自适应模块：Progressive Networks（Rusu et al. 2016）、Mixture-of-Experts 路由（Shazeer et al. 2017）、PackNet（Mallya & Lazebnik 2018）、带 adapter 的冻结骨干。保留由架构强制：冻结的模块无法被覆盖。

Limit here在终身学习里的局限 Growing a module per regime is unsustainable; fixed-capacity designs must decide the size of the core up front.每个体制长一个模块不可持续；定容设计必须提前决定核心有多大。

Way in改造路径 Fixed-capacity mixture of experts: always-active core experts plus dynamically routed regime-specific experts, with the core budget as a hyper-parameter governed by the core-share trade-off.定容 MoE：始终激活的核心专家加动态路由的体制专用专家，核心预算是超参数，受核心占比的权衡约束。

B-3

School B-3: memory-based retention流派 B-3：基于记忆的保留

What it is是什么 Store core information in an external retrieval system: memory networks, retrieval-augmented transformers (Borgeaud et al. 2022), long-context in-context learning. Storage and compute decouple; adding a memory entry does not disturb the others.把核心信息存进外部检索系统：memory networks、检索增强的 transformer（Borgeaud et al. 2022）、长上下文 ICL。存储与计算解耦；新增条目不扰动已有条目。

Limit here在终身学习里的局限 Retrieval costs compute; large stores retrieve noisily; telling core memories from peripheral ones needs an internal value function.检索消耗算力；大规模记忆库检索有噪声；区分核心记忆与外围记忆需要内部价值函数。

Way in改造路径 Two tiers: a small always-retrieved core store and a large on-demand peripheral store, with periodic consolidation moving entries between them by retrieval frequency and downstream impact.分层记忆：小规模永远检索的核心记忆，加大规模按需查询的外围记忆；周期性 consolidation 按检索频率与下游影响在两层间迁移条目。

B-4

School B-4: replay-based retention流派 B-4：基于重放的保留

What it is是什么 Rehearse past experience while learning, literally (experience replay, Rolnick et al. 2019) or through a generative model (generative replay, Shin et al. 2017). Conceptually simple, empirically strong on continual-learning benchmarks.学习时复习过往经验，要么字面复习（Experience Replay，Rolnick et al. 2019），要么通过生成模型（Generative Replay，Shin et al. 2017）。概念简单，在持续学习基准上经验表现强。

Limit here在终身学习里的局限 Needs stored or generated past data, with privacy, storage and fidelity issues; generative replay drifts over long horizons.需要存储或生成过往数据，有隐私、存储、保真度问题；生成重放在长视界下会漂移。

Way in改造路径 Selective replay of the experience that matters for the core set, using the counterfactual-importance estimate of school B-1.基于 B-1 的反事实重要性估计，只对核心集相关的经验做选择性重放。

S3.5b

Benchmark need: a marked core基准需求：标出核心

The designer must mark which knowledge is core, meant to survive every regime, and which is replaceable; otherwise retention cannot be scored.

设计者必须标出哪些知识是核心、应当跨越所有体制存活，哪些是可替换的；否则保留无法计分。

D1.11

Cumulative dynamic regret累积动态遗憾

\[\mathrm{Regret}_T(\pi) := \mathbb E_{\mu_\cdot \sim \mathcal M_\rho}\ \mathbb E_{\{\Phi^{(k)}\} \sim \mu_\cdot}\Big[\sum_{k=1}^{T} \big(R^*_{BC}(\Phi^{(k)}) - R_k(\pi)\big)\Big]\]

How far the agent falls short of the bounded-capacity oracle, regime by regime, summed over the horizon and averaged over the generating process.

智能体逐体制比有界容量预言机少拿多少，沿视界累加，再对生成过程取平均。

In plain words直白地说 We measure how many points our student scored below the ideal student, added up over the whole journey. The total shortfall is the regret.我们度量我们的学生比理想学生少拿了多少分，整个旅程累加起来。总亏空就是 regret。

Lineage来源 Regret as the performance measure is online learning (Cesa-Bianchi & Lugosi 2006); comparing against a moving target is dynamic regret (Besbes, Gur & Zeevi 2015).以 regret 为性能度量是在线学习（Cesa-Bianchi & Lugosi 2006）；对照移动目标是动态 regret（Besbes, Gur & Zeevi 2015）。

P2.2

One-line characterisation一句话刻画

\[\text{LLL} \;=\; \underbrace{\text{non-stationary RL under bounded variation}}_{C1 \wedge C2} \;\cap\; \underbrace{\text{constrained learning with core-information retention}}_{C3}\]

The shortest discriminating statement: reinforcement learning in a world whose drift has a sub-linear budget, intersected with learning under a retention requirement. Either half alone is an existing paradigm.

最短的判别式陈述：在漂移有亚线性预算的世界里做强化学习，交上带保留要求的学习。任一半单独拿出来都是已有范式。

In plain words直白地说 Lifelong learning is "reinforcement learning in a changing world" plus "never lose your core identity". Having only one of the two is not lifelong learning.终身学习 = "变化世界中的强化学习" + "绝不失去核心身份"。只有其中一个的都不是终身学习。
S3.6

Research programme研究纲领

Three independent contributions. Theory: regret upper and lower bounds for each combination of schools, as functions of drift rate, core share and identifiability, which extends non-stationary optimisation by the core-information dimension. Algorithms: joint training procedures that handle the three challenges together, the coupling being the difficulty. Empirics: the parameterised benchmark, evaluating schools along the three difficulty dimensions and reporting regret decomposed into an information gap and an execution gap.

三项独立贡献。理论：作为漂移速度、核心占比、可识别性的函数，推导各流派组合的 regret 上下界，把非平稳优化理论沿核心信息维度扩展。算法：同时处理三个挑战的联合训练程序，难点在模块耦合。实证：参数化基准，沿三个难度维度评估各流派，并把 regret 拆成信息缺口与执行缺口来报告。

In plain words直白地说 This is not a finished theory. It is a map that says where the work is: pure mathematics, algorithm design, and empirical measurement, each enough for a direction of its own.这不是完工的理论，是一张地图，指出工作所在：纯数学、算法设计、实证测量，每一项都足够撑起一个方向。
D1.12

The lifelong-learning problem终身学习问题

\[\pi^{LLL} := \arg\min_{\pi \in \Pi_{N,C}} \mathrm{Regret}_T(\pi)\]

Given the environment level, the resource constraint and the core set, find the policy in the constrained class that minimises regret. No explicit constraint is written: losing core information makes regret diverge, so any minimiser keeps it.

给定环境层级、资源约束和核心集，在约束类里找 regret 最小的策略。不写显式约束：丢核心信息会让 regret 发散，所以任何最小化者都会保住它。

In plain words直白地说 The whole problem: design a student whose total shortfall stays as small as possible, however long the journey. That student must remember what truly matters, because forgetting it makes the shortfall explode later.整个问题就是：设计一个学生，让总亏空尽量小，旅途多长都挺得住。这个学生必须记住真正重要的东西，因为忘了它会让亏空在未来爆掉。

Lineage来源 Constrained MDPs (Altman 1999) are where a retention requirement would normally be a constraint; here it is internalised through divergence.在约束 MDP（Altman 1999）里，保留要求本会写成约束；这里通过发散把它内化了。

D2.1

Criterion definition of lifelong learning终身学习的判据式定义

\[\text{a learning problem is lifelong learning} \iff C1 \wedge C2 \wedge C3\]

All three criteria, jointly. Applied to the neighbouring paradigms (table in the Boundaries section): transfer learning fails C2, batch meta-learning fails C1, Bayesian RL and online meta-learning fail C2 and C3, non-stationary RL fails C3, general continual RL does not require C2 or C3 and is a strict superset, and continual learning is partly inside depending on its benchmark's budget.

三条同时成立。对相邻范式逐一套用（见"边界"一节的表）：迁移学习不满足 C2；批量元学习不满足 C1；贝叶斯强化学习与在线元学习不满足 C2 和 C3；非平稳强化学习不满足 C3；一般化持续强化学习不要求 C2 与 C3，是真超集；持续学习视其基准的预算而定，部分属于。

In plain words直白地说 Many problems look like lifelong learning from the outside. The three criteria are a checklist: fail one and it is something else.很多学习问题表面上像终身学习。三条判据是一张清单：有一条不满足，它就是别的东西。
S3.4

Three-module architecture三模块架构

A complete agent must answer all three challenges at once; picking one school per challenge gives a family of architectures. The diagram in the section below shows a typical member: a frozen core module that always runs and carries the core set (B-2), an adaptive regime-specific module (C-3 with C-4), and a regime identifier (A-1 with A-2) that gates the routing. The three modules have three coupled meta-objectives; training them jointly is open, with bilevel optimisation and two-time-scale stochastic approximation as candidates.

完整的智能体必须同时回答三个挑战；每个挑战选一个流派，合起来是一族架构。下一节的图给出一个典型成员：始终运行、承载核心集的冻结核心模块（B-2），体制专用的自适应模块（C-3 加 C-4），以及门控路由的体制识别器（A-1 加 A-2）。三个模块有三个耦合的元目标；联合训练是开放问题，双层优化和双时间尺度随机逼近是候选。

In plain words直白地说 A real system needs three things at once: a core that does not change, a flexible part that adapts, and a detector that knows when to switch the flexible part. The hard bit is training the three together without them getting in each other's way.真实系统同时需要三件事：一个不变的核心、一个灵活的自适应部分、一个知道何时切换自适应部分的检测器。难点是让三者协同训练而不互相干扰。
S3.5

What a benchmark must provide基准必须提供什么

No existing benchmark satisfies all three criteria. One that did would need a parameterised variation budget (a knob from near-stationary to near Level 0), a known or estimable core set (which knowledge is meant to survive every regime), a computable approximation of the oracle (a reference model of the same size, handed the regime), and a horizon of hundreds to thousands of switches. Meta-World, Continual World and Procgen each cover part of this; none parameterises the budget or marks the core. Building the first true benchmark is a research contribution in itself.

没有现成基准同时满足三条判据。一个合格的基准需要：参数化的变动预算（从近平稳到接近第 0 级的旋钮）、已知或可估计的核心集（哪些知识应当跨越所有体制存活）、可计算的预言机近似（同规模、被告知体制的参考模型）、数百到数千次切换的视界。Meta-World、Continual World、Procgen 各覆盖一部分；没有一个参数化预算或标出核心。构造第一个真正的基准本身就是研究贡献。

In plain words直白地说 There is no standard exam for lifelong learning yet. Existing tests each check part of it. Writing a real exam, with a dial for how fast the world changes, a list of which knowledge is essential and a reference score, is a project on its own.目前还没有终身学习的标准考试。已有的测试只测其中一部分。构造一个真正的考试，带调节世界变化速度的旋钮、标注哪些知识必要、附带参考分数，本身就是一个独立的研究项目。
C1

Criterion C1: order matters判据 C1：时间序列性

\[\mathbb E\big[R_k(\pi;\ \mathcal I_{agent}(t_k))\big] \text{ depends on } \mathcal I_{agent}(t_k), \text{ itself fixed by the history up to } t_k\]

There is a time index along which samples or tasks cannot be freely reordered: what the agent knows at a given time is a function of everything before it, and reward depends on that.

存在一个时间索引，样本或任务的处理顺序沿着它不可自由交换：智能体在某一时刻知道什么，是此前一切的函数，而奖励依赖于此。

In plain words直白地说 Time is real. You cannot shuffle the order in which things happen: the student at step ten was shaped by the first nine steps.时间有意义。你不能打乱事情发生的顺序：第十步的学生是由前九步塑造出来的。

Lineage来源 Shared by all online learning; batch meta-learning violates it by allowing task order to be permuted.所有在线学习都满足；批量元学习允许任务顺序交换，因此违反它。

C2

Criterion C2: structured non-stationarity判据 C2：结构化非平稳

\[V_T(\mu_\cdot) > 0 \qquad \text{and} \qquad V_T(\mu_\cdot) = o(T)\]

The generating distribution moves, but its total movement grows sub-linearly in the horizon. This places the problem at Level 1, or on its boundary with Level 2.

生成分布在动，但总动量随视界亚线性增长。这把问题放在第 1 级，或第 1 级与第 2 级的边界上。

In plain words直白地说 The world keeps changing, but not madly. There is always enough consistency to learn from, and never enough to learn once and stop.世界一直在变，但不是疯狂地变。总有足够的一致性可供学习，但也从不足以学一次就停。

Lineage来源 Besbes, Gur & Zeevi 2015. Bayesian RL and online meta-learning usually assume zero budget; standard continual-learning benchmarks often impose near-linear budgets, which puts them outside.Besbes, Gur & Zeevi 2015。贝叶斯强化学习和在线元学习通常假设预算为零；常见持续学习基准常施加近线性预算，因此落在外面。

C3

Criterion C3: a non-empty core判据 C3：核心信息非空

\[\mathcal I_{core} \neq \emptyset\]

There exists knowledge whose loss makes regret diverge. Without such knowledge there is nothing that has to be carried across changes, and the problem is just non-stationary RL.

存在丢失后会使 regret 发散的知识。没有这种知识，就没有必须跨越变化带下去的东西，问题就只是非平稳强化学习。

In plain words直白地说 Something has to be carried through every change, or the student eventually becomes useless. If your problem has no such thing, you do not have a lifelong-learning problem.有些东西必须贯穿每一次变化被带下去，否则学生最终会变得没用。如果你的问题里不存在这种东西，那你就没有终身学习问题。

Lineage来源 Constrained learning (Altman 1999) and the perfect-memory result of Knoblauch, Husain & Diethe 2020; non-stationary RL has no such object.约束学习（Altman 1999）和 Knoblauch, Husain & Diethe 2020 的完美记忆结果；非平稳强化学习没有这个对象。

D2.1

Criterion definition of lifelong learning终身学习的判据式定义

\[\text{a learning problem is lifelong learning} \iff C1 \wedge C2 \wedge C3\]

All three criteria, jointly. Applied to the neighbouring paradigms (table in the Boundaries section): transfer learning fails C2, batch meta-learning fails C1, Bayesian RL and online meta-learning fail C2 and C3, non-stationary RL fails C3, general continual RL does not require C2 or C3 and is a strict superset, and continual learning is partly inside depending on its benchmark's budget.

三条同时成立。对相邻范式逐一套用（见"边界"一节的表）：迁移学习不满足 C2；批量元学习不满足 C1；贝叶斯强化学习与在线元学习不满足 C2 和 C3；非平稳强化学习不满足 C3；一般化持续强化学习不要求 C2 与 C3，是真超集；持续学习视其基准的预算而定，部分属于。

In plain words直白地说 Many problems look like lifelong learning from the outside. The three criteria are a checklist: fail one and it is something else.很多学习问题表面上像终身学习。三条判据是一张清单：有一条不满足，它就是别的东西。

What the boundary does not say边界不涉及的

The criteria classify; they do not grade difficulty. Three dimensions do: how fast the budget grows, what share of capacity the core set occupies, and how many observations it takes to identify the regime. Their trade-offs are open, and the schools take different positions on them.
