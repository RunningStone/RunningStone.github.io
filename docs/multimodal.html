Multimodal & Virtual Cell

A single question runs through this work: when an experiment is too expensive to run at scale, can a model stand in for it? First for spatial transcriptomics — a £7,000+ assay replaced by a routine H&E slide — then for cellular dynamics itself: generative models that simulate the trajectory a cell would take.

The work sits in the Secrier Lab (Computational Cancer Biology, UCL Genetics Institute), whose programme is somatic evolution in cancer — how mutational processes shape cellular plasticity and stress response, and how cells and their microenvironment co-evolve under therapy resistance, with tumour dormancy supported by a UKRI Future Leaders Fellowship. My contribution is the modelling layer: I was the sole AI expert in the group, responsible for all AI infrastructure, tool development, and server management.

1 · Pathology vision–language models → spatial transcriptomics

Trained and fine-tuned multimodal foundation models on gigapixel histopathology images to predict gene expression profiles, effectively replacing £7,000+ spatial transcriptomics experiments with routine H&E slides. Validated that the inferred spatial population structures match ST ground truth, enabling studies previously blocked by budget.

  • PEKA — parameter-efficient knowledge transfer
    Accepted at MICCAI 2025 as top 9%.
    Paper · MICCAICode
  • Few-shot adaptation of general vision models
    Follow-up to the MICCAI work: a general-domain vision foundation model, adapted with only a small amount of pathology data, matches or exceeds pathology-specialised models (e.g. UNI) on gene-expression prediction from H&E. (Under revision.)

2 · Single-cell foundation models & cellular dynamics

Where the pathology work infers a static molecular state, this line models the transition between states — the lab's question of cell-state plasticity, posed as a generative modelling problem with guided cellular state design as the goal.

  • ODE / flow-matching single-cell foundation model
    A generative model based on ordinary differential equations and flow matching to capture temporal cell-state transitions and causal disentanglement; enables guided trajectory design, e.g. inducing specific differentiation paths from a given initial state. Currently under wet-lab validation with collaborators.
  • Generative dormancy trajectories (scFM + Schrödinger bridge)
    Extended the dynamics work to tumour-cell dormancy: a generative model coupling a single-cell foundation model with a Schrödinger-bridge formulation that, given a non-dormant cell, generates the full entry-into-dormancy trajectory. Validation experiments in progress.
  • Evolution-aware cell prototypes
    Introduced temporal / cell-evolution-order supervision into single-cell foundation models by learning a prototype-cell dictionary, yielding prototypes tied to evolutionary trajectories. Addresses scFM's weakness in defining novel cell populations relative to classical bioinformatics workflows. (Under revision.)

3 · Transcriptomic language modelling

Treating transcriptomes as a language a model can be fine-tuned to read — which surfaces biomarkers that classical differential-expression pipelines miss.

  • EMT state classification with LLMs
    Fine-tuned large language models to decode the transcriptomic 'language' underlying epithelial–mesenchymal transition and tumour dormancy, leading to novel biomarkers and therapeutic targets. Under revision at Nature Communications.
    Paper · bioRxiv
  • Foundation-model alignment with dynamical systems
    Integrated multimodal foundation models with cell dynamic systems (ODE/SDE formulations) to model the tumour microenvironment and dormancy mechanisms; contributed to a multi-author study under review at Nature Cancer.
    Paper · bioRxiv

4 · Agents for bioinformatics

The agent line and the biology line meet here — this is where RL post-training first became a practical tool for me rather than a topic.

  • RL-enhanced agent for cell-type classification
    An autonomous agent that retrieves dormancy markers from the literature and executes single-cell validation pipelines; proposed an accompanying benchmark and applied post-training reinforcement learning to improve performance.
